Internal (skills): add `epic-em` (FIX-1073) — a standing engineering-manager posture over `epic-lifecycle` for running an epic without bottlenecking on the user's approval. It composes `epic-lifecycle` and `docs/contributing/asking-for-decisions.md` rather than restating them, adds no state and no step, and **moves no gate**: the epic-objective gate, every per-issue spec approval, and every merge stay the user's, because an absorbed spec gate only relocates unreviewed direction to the merge gate (the worst moment to change one) and an absorbed merge gate puts unreviewed code on `main`. What changes is the traffic between the gates — an escalated *engineering* blocker skips step 4's surfacing and is decided and recorded through the existing `blockerResolutions` field, with only a critical product or architectural call going up; the PR-feedback cap's four engineering answers are taken directly while "merge as-is with a known gap" still goes to the user; and a wake leads with outcomes rather than a phase table. Three things are explicitly out of scope for absorption: an `INCONCLUSIVE` POC verdict and an epic-level `unsettled` claim stay the user's (an evidence run that failed to settle a question is exactly when it stops being an engineering call), and the EM decides from what a worker reported — when the answer isn't there it dispatches a worker to get it, never opening a spec or a diff in the coordinator (`orchestration.md` → "The coordinator dispatches; it never does the work"). Registered in `CLAUDE.md` → Workflow skills with a one-line back-link from `epic-lifecycle`'s Boundaries; `docs/contributing/orchestration.md` is deliberately untouched, since `epic-em` is a posture and not a third lifecycle.
