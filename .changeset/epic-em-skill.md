Internal (skills): add `epic-em` (FIX-1073) — a thin engineering-manager posture over `epic-lifecycle` for running an epic without bottlenecking on the user's approval. It composes `epic-lifecycle` and `docs/contributing/asking-for-decisions.md` rather than restating them, adds no state and no step, and **moves no gate**: the epic-objective gate, every per-issue spec approval, and every merge stay the user's, because an absorbed spec gate only relocates unreviewed direction to the merge gate (the worst moment to change one) and an absorbed merge gate puts unreviewed code on `main`. What it does change is the traffic between the gates — an escalated engineering fork is decided in-session and recorded via the existing `blockerResolutions` field, with only a critical product or architectural call going up; the PR-feedback cap's four engineering answers are taken directly and only "merge as-is with a known gap" is surfaced; and the wake's report leads with outcomes rather than a phase table (a framing change over material the coordinator already holds, not a licence to read diffs). Registered in `CLAUDE.md` → Workflow skills; `docs/contributing/orchestration.md` is deliberately untouched, since `epic-em` is a posture and not a third lifecycle.
