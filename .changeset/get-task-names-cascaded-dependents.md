`getTask` names the dependents a failure or a cancel cancelled when its answer is how the filer hears that ending: a wait that finds the task already ended, a wait the ending resumes, or a read of an ending held for a wait that lapsed. The answer carries `cancelled` beside `task`, as the notice it replaces would have. Before, the notice was dropped and nothing named them (FIX-2138).
