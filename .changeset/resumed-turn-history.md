A tool call that waited on an approval no longer reads as failed. A later turn's history replays it once, with its result; before, it also replayed the wait as a failed call carrying the approval's question, so the model read it as a failure it had retried. `isSuspensionWait(item)` (from `@flow-state-dev/contracts/items`, re-exported by `@flow-state-dev/core/items`) tells such a wait from a real failure, and the registry's tool card draws it as awaiting approval. Shift Manager draws a waiting call as waiting, and a conversation redraws once its ask is answered on the page, again when the run it resumed stops (FIX-1901).
