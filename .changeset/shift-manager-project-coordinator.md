A project's Stream is your own project coordinator, its Workstreams tab lists every workstream's entry with the project's progress (your own opens on its lead's conversation, anyone else's on its details and latest report), and its Board shows only your own workstreams' tasks. PROJECTS lists shared projects and your private ones, and opening a workstream gives it a coordinator of its own on your roster as its lead. The DevTeam profile installs `project-coordinator` and `workstream-coordinator`, and its chief of staff can open a workstream (FIX-1793).
