A project's Stream is now your own project coordinator, and its Workstreams tab lists every workstream with the project's progress. You open a workstream by asking your project coordinator for the work: it picks the lead from your workers, such as the DevTeam's engineering manager, and hands your ask to it. The Workstreams tab has no form for opening one, and the DevTeam profile has no workstream coordinator worker. The Tasks view groups a workstream's tasks under the workstream, so one worker leading two workstreams shows as two groups (FIX-1793).
