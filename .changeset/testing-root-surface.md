**Breaking:** the `@flow-state-dev/testing` root now holds what testing an app needs; benchmarking moves to `@flow-state-dev/testing/benchmark`, and `testBlock` covers sequencers and routers (FIX-2460).

- `runBenchmark`, `comparePatterns`, `baselineSubject`, `defineBenchmark`, `buildBenchmarkReport`, `renderScorecard`, `estimateCostUsd` and their types (`RunBenchmarkConfig`, `ComparePatternsConfig`, `SubjectCategoryStat`, `BenchmarkRanking`, `BenchmarkReport`, `BenchmarkRunResult`, `BenchmarkDefinition`, `BuildBenchmarkReportMeta`) are exported from `@flow-state-dev/testing/benchmark` only. So is the benchmark contract that was on `@flow-state-dev/core`: `BenchmarkTask`, `BenchmarkCategory`, `BenchmarkSubject`, `BenchmarkAdapter`, `BenchmarkAdapterOptions`, `BenchmarkRegistry`. There are no aliases: an old import fails to compile with "has no exported member"; change it to `from "@flow-state-dev/testing/benchmark"`. `fsdev benchmark` runs as before.
- `testSequencer` and `testRouter` are removed, with `TestSequencerResult` and `TestRouterResult`. Call `testBlock` instead: a sequencer's result carries `steps`, `sideChainResults` and `loopIterations`, typed when the block is a `SequencerDefinition`, and a router's carries `selectedRoute`, which is now absent (not `"unknown"`) when no route was chosen.
- `findImportsFromEntry`, `findNodeBuiltinsFromEntry` and `FindNodeBuiltinsOptions` are no longer published.

Docs: packages/testing/README.md (the benchmark section imports from `@flow-state-dev/testing/benchmark`; list the public subpaths `testing/benchmark` and `testing/conformance`; `testBlock` replaces `testSequencer` and `testRouter`, with `steps`, `sideChainResults`, `loopIterations` and `selectedRoute` on its result; drop the scanners), apps/docs/docs/api/testing.md (same `testBlock` change; drop `testSequencer`, `testRouter`, their result types and the benchmark names from the root's list), apps/docs/docs/testing/overview.md and apps/docs/docs/testing/flow-integration-tests.md (`testBlock` for a sequencer or a router), apps/docs/docs/testing/benchmarks.md, apps/docs/docs/api/benchmarks.md and apps/docs/guides/choosing-patterns-with-benchmarks.md (import the harness and `BenchmarkTask` from `@flow-state-dev/testing/benchmark`, the registry from `@flow-state-dev/patterns/benchmark`), apps/docs/docs/api/package-roots.md (testing's row: the root is for testing an app and evals; its public subpaths are `testing/benchmark` and `testing/conformance`)
