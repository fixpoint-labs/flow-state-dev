A worker flow now declares its roles where it is registered, `workerFlow(build, { roles })` or `{ flow, roles }` in `workerFlows`: `contributor` (takes tasks on `work`, from `workerTaskEntry`), `manager` (files tasks and runs its board on `dispatch`, with `onTaskSettled`, from `defineSessionBoard`) and `coordinator`. Every hire path refuses a declared role whose entries are missing, weren't built by its factory or refuse their input, and a role factory's piece on a flow that doesn't declare the role, naming the flow, the role, the entry and the fix; the built-in `agent` and `coordinator` declare their own. A flow that spreads `workerTaskEntry` or `defineSessionBoard`'s pieces without declaring the role is refused until it does. Every worker flow's door is now `respond`: the built-in `agent`'s, the coordinator's and the Error Reporter's `run`, and any worker flow's door under another name, are renamed, and a worker flow whose message action has another name is refused, naming the action to rename; apps that send to the inventory's `door` change nothing. A board's pass entry `runTaskBoard` is now `dispatch`. Neither old name is kept as an alias: call `upgradeWorkerEntryNames(runtime.stores, flows, { incarnationOf: resolveRequestIncarnation })` once at host start, with `hireWorkforce`'s copies, before `getRouter()`, so a request an earlier version left unfinished under `run` or `message` resumes as `respond`, and a request or wake under `runTaskBoard` as `dispatch`. Drain BullMQ queues (or let them empty) before deploying this version: a job queued under an old action name fails. `workerFlowProblems` takes the roles as a third argument. Teams outside the repo rename their doors and declare their roles at upgrade (FIX-2084).
