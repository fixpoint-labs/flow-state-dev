Workers on the built-in `agent`, `coordinator` and error-reporter flows now summarize older turns of a long conversation by default, so a decision made early is still used after those turns leave the history window (FIX-2500).

- **New default, the history summary:** before a worker answers, turns about to leave the history window (50 turns by default) are folded into a running summary, which the worker reads as an `<earlier-conversation>` section after its prompt, with only the turns after the summary read word for word. It is core's `historySummary`, with a summarizer told to keep every name, date, code and number word for word and to replace the summary so far rather than append to it. The resource is the session's `historySummary`, as before for an app that wired it.
- **What it costs:** a short conversation pays one store read per turn and no model call. Past about 38 turns, one extra model call per 12 turns (a quarter of the window), on the worker's own model (the one it answers with), reading up to 12 turns, documents included. A failed fold is a warning; the turn still answers, and the next turn retries.
- **Turning it off:** `defaults: { historySummary: false }` in a worker's `WORKER.md` stops the fold and the summary for that worker; past the window, old turns drop out as before. `defaults: false` turns it off along with the date block and the skills index, so a long conversation loses its early turns again. There is no app-wide switch: turn it off worker by worker. Switched off after folding, the summary leaves the prompt and the whole window is read again; the stored summary is kept.
- **An app's own summary replaces it:** an app that passes its own `utility.historySummary(...)` capability in `defineAgentWorkerFlow({ uses })` (with its fold in `beforeAnswer`) keeps its summary, and the default steps aside: no second fold, no second section. It is recognised by core's `historySummaryStateSchema` on a static `uses` entry; a summary passed as a dynamic `uses` entry, or one not built from core's `historySummary`, is not seen and runs beside the default.
- **A conversation already past its window** when this ships folds on its next turn and marks the summary incomplete (`complete: false`): the turns already gone are not recovered.
