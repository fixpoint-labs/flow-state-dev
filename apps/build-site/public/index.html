Why this is different

Other assistants build inside their room. Flow State lets you rebuild the room.

A great assistant

Makes things for you

  • Answers and documents appear inside its chat
  • The app looks the same for everyone
  • Your way of working bends to the tool
A workspace that becomes yours

Becomes the thing you need

  • Screens, sidebar and home page change when you ask
  • Helpers keep working while you're away
  • The tool bends to the way you work

Start from something that works, then say what you'd change. There's no setup to learn, no template to pick, and nothing technical to open.

A coachPut each client in the sidebar and prep me before every session.

A section per client, with notes and a prep brief ready before each call.

A shop ownerStart my day with what sold, what's low, and who's waiting on a reply.

A morning screen instead of five tabs, and a helper that drafts the replies.

A writerHide everything except today's chapter and my research.

A quieter app with fewer buttons, shaped around deep work.

The idea

Software made for an audience of one. You.

Everyone will work with AI. The app you work in should fit you as well as the work does. Flow State is open source, so the world can find better ways to work instead of waiting for one company to ship them.
