Product horizon

Three jobs, one substrate

This is the public product horizon. Not a company plan. FSD is three jobs, not two products: an unopinionated framework, a set of opinions other people can take as a component, and one user product that drives coding harnesses. The sibling atlases design the last two. This page keeps the horizon from flattening them into a pair of installs.

exists — the shape shown is the real one proposed — design, not built

Sibling atlases: Conductor — the user-product design. Workforce — the opinions layer. Neither page is rewritten here.

01

Three jobs

A framework other people build on. Opinions they can take without inventing their own. One user product we are dogfooding. Those are different jobs. Drawing the last two as products people install is the mistake this page exists to prevent.

Layer 1
unopinionated framework

The substrate. Four block kinds, flows, sessions, resources, capabilities, the board, skills. Releasing. Not on the market. Other people can build on it. It can land inside other products.

Workforce
opinions / component

An opinionated open-source extension of Layer 1 so authors get going. Releasable as a component. Not a user product. Not something people install beside Conductor.

Conductor
the user-product focus

A software-dev meta-harness that drives coding harnesses. Unpublished lab. Session owned by the workflow is its opinion, not Layer 1 law.

02

Who sits on whom

Layer 1 has substrate opinions only. Workforce packages a way to start. Conductor consumes both and adds a coding-product opinion. A new box on this figure is an opinion or a product, never a fifth block kind.

Do not merge the harnesses

The Conductor atlas and the Workforce atlas stay separate designs. Conductor may own “a session belongs to the workflow.” Layer 1 may not. Workforce collaboration later talks through the same Relay door Conductor uses. This page triangulates the horizon. It does not settle either atlas’s open threads.

03

This cycle

Prove Layer 1. Keep Conductor an unpublished lab until dogfood holds. Ship Workforce as opinions and a component, not as a user product. Collaboration is off this floor.

07

What stays held

A horizon page that starts inventing primitives has failed. These stay off the floor on purpose.

Do not invent a Layer 1 primitive

Collaboration is a use of Relay, not a fifth block kind, not a second bus, not a new scope. If the door is missing a field, that is a Layer 1 gap on the existing door. It is not a Workforce package.

Do not lift session-owned-by-workflow into Layer 1

That sentence is a Conductor opinion. A session already carries a flowKind and nothing more binding. Bake workflow-ownership into the substrate and a later agent-owned channel is designed out.

Do not draw Workforce as a second product

Today it is opinions and a component. A user-product peel-off is a later maybe. The opinions stay the shared floor either way. Two install boxes on a slide is the lie this page is for.

Do not date Conductor

It stays unpublished until dogfood holds. Facing the world is a later clock with no calendar attached. A date invented here would be a promise the lab has not earned.

The one-sentence version

Prove the framework; keep the coding product in the lab; keep Workforce a component; open collaboration later as wait on the one Relay door. Three jobs. One substrate. No second product hiding in the middle.
