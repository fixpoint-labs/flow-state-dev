The figure rules are beside it, in [`user-docs-figures.md`](../user-docs-figures.md).

What to write next is the page plan,
[`specs/epics/FIX-2696/SITEMAP/`](../../../specs/epics/FIX-2696/SITEMAP/README.md): every page of
the new site, with its contents, its cold-read questions, a brief for each figure, its examples,
the Reference pages it links to and where its facts are today. Write Start and Concepts in its
reading order. A page's entry is the starting point for its fact sheet (pass 1) and its cold-read
questions (pass 2), and a fact sheet may cite only what the standard allows, whatever an entry's
Sources line names.
