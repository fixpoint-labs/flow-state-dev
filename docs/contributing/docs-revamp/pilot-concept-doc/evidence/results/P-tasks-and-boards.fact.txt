## 3. RIGHT claims

I counted 56 claims as RIGHT. Ten representative ones:

1. The six statuses are `pending`, `in_progress`, `held`, `completed`, `errored` and `cancelled`, and `completed` has no way back (`tasks/schema/task-status.ts:77-84`).
2. Status `blocked` reads as `held` (`task-status.ts:60`, `retired-shapes.ts:49-57`).
3. Four fields never reach a browser: `claimedBy`, `createdBy`, `filer`, `filedUnder` (`tasks/collection/change-event.ts:140`). The lease deadline isn't among them.
4. The lease is two minutes (`tasks/collection/internal.ts:1131`, `DEFAULT_LEASE_DURATION_MS = 120_000`). Recovery is bounded at three, and the fourth lapse settles the task as errored (`internal.ts:524,1093-1095`).
5. Default caps are 500 total tasks, 100 pending adds and 50 retries (`tasks/collection/task-caps.ts:221,224,243`). A board only applies them to a collection it builds, and passing caps with a supplied collection throws (`task-board/index.ts:1395-1411`).
6. Claim orders are `fifo` (the default) and `priority` (highest first, ties by oldest `createdAt`), with no `topological` (`task-board/shared.ts:27-38`, `claim-orders/priority.ts`). The glossary does say `topological` is the default (`apps/docs/docs/glossary.md:89`).
7. Default board concurrency is 4 (`task-board/index.ts:402-405`). Worker-failure policy is `"skip"` or `"fail"`, with `skip` the default (`index.ts:495-501`). `onIdle` defaults to `complete-or-blocked`.
8. Steers and open questions share a cap of 20 (`tasks/schema/steer.ts:10`, `tasks/steers.ts:83`).
9. The task tools are `createTask`, `getTask`, `listTasks`, `updateTask` and `reportProgress` (`skills/task-tools-capability.ts:679,1362,1549,1888,1945`). `updateTask` refuses changes to filing settings, and cancelling is sent alone.
10. Hold kinds are question, confirm, external (an event wait) and a run's suspension. A confirm's No either cancels or continues (`tasks/schema/hold.ts:99-116`). Board modes are `"plan"` and `"delegation"`, and `goalSeekLoop` refuses a delegation board (`goal-seek-loop.ts:343`).
