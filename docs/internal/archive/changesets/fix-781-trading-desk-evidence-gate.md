FIX-781: Add an always-on, deterministic **evidence-sufficiency gate** to the private `@flow-state-dev/trading-desk` example. The portfolio-manager commit now caps new exposure whenever the evidence behind a call is too thin — an absent or thin valuation spine, an absent or thin reward-to-risk figure, or an unavailable primary financial input — independent of the optional FIX-752 risk mandate, so it fires on mandate-blind and portfolio-blind runs alike. On insufficient evidence the target is capped to the current position (`min(target, currentWeight)`; a held-but-unpriced name skips the numeric clamp and relies on the action downgrade, the FIX-761 policy-gate precedent), `initiate`/`add` become `hold`, and `trim`/`exit`/`hold` are preserved; the gate never touches `finalRating` (the FIX-715/752/761 orthogonality). The verdict + clamp flags mirror onto the PM memo, the decision snapshot, and the RunSummary, and a matching `evidence/*` deterministic invariant recomputes the verdict and asserts the no-add held. No publishable package surface changes.
