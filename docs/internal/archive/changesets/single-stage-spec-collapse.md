Internal (docs/skills): Part II of a spec (the Build Plan) is now **directional** — shape, layers, and sequence carried by a mermaid diagram, not an exhaustive blueprint of signatures and file trees; the implementer owns the exact code. Because a directional Part II is cheap to write, the two-stage draft-gated flow collapses: `issue-spec` authors Part I and Part II together and opens the spec PR **ready for review** at a single spec-approval gate. Orchestrators, the spec template, `orchestration.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and `CLAUDE.md` are updated to match.
