## Summary

**The problem.** Look-through — seeing inside an ETF so that Apple held directly
and Apple held through SPY add up — is arithmetically correct but produced an
unusable screen. Against a real 44-ETF book it shows two sector panels that
appear to disagree, a holdings table that silently hides most of its rows, a stat
reading `1.5–338.1`, and forty funds mashed into one run-on sentence.

**What we're building — two things, nothing else.** Both come straight from what
the user asked for:

1. **One sector view covering 100% of invested NAV**, with the portion we can't
   see inside drawn as an explicit bar rather than left as a silent gap.
   → *"Sector exposure is supposed to ultimately allow me to see exposure of the
   entire portfolio."*
2. **One holdings view** giving per-symbol totals aggregated across every wrapper
   and every account. → *"how much of each stock I own, regardless if it's in an
   ETF or directly owned across multiple accounts."*

**What we're deliberately not building** (§6): sector→symbol drill-down, a basis
toggle, composition bars, a fine-grained residual taxonomy, and the `Effective
positions` statistic. Each is defensible; none is necessary for the two outcomes,
and together they roughly tripled the plan.

**The key finding** (§0.1): the two sector panels people read as contradictory
are already on the **same denominator**. The look-through bars simply stop short
of 100% because the remainder is never drawn. This is a missing bar, not broken
math — which is why outcome 1 is mostly arithmetic already done.

**Shape of the work:** Phase 1 is presentation-only, no leaf change, ~half a day,
and fixes the "numbers don't add up" perception on its own — it can ship
independently and early. Phase 2 adds four additive leaf fields and the unified
panel, ~3 days. Both phases are in scope for this issue; the split exists because
Phase 1 needs no new data and shouldn't wait behind a contract change.

## Baseline

**Baseline is `main` at `947ad9d61`** — the merge of FIX-801 sub-PR c (#927),
which completed the look-through feature. Everything this spec depends on is now
on `main`; an earlier draft of this document was written pre-merge and carried a
caveat that no longer applies.

The specific symbols and behaviours the plan builds on, all present as of that
commit: `MUTUAL_FUND_ATTRIBUTION_SUPPRESSED_REASON` ·
`FIXED_INCOME_ATTRIBUTION_SUPPRESSED_REASON` ·
`CONSTITUENT_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE_REASON` ·
`excludeFixedIncomeFromProfileMap`'s sentinel seeding · `appendLookThroughLines` ·
`buildPortfolioContext` receiving an ETF-profile map.

**Consequence:** Phase 1's "no leaf change" claim holds on this baseline. It
would *not* have held before #927 merged — without those constants there is
nothing to classify reasons against, and without the sentinel seeding the curated
bond ETFs fall through to the leaf's `"no stored profile"` branch and become
indistinguishable from unfetched funds. That constraint is now resolved.

**Verified: policy exclusions *are* distinguishable by reason value on #927.**
`excludeFixedIncomeFromProfileMap` seeds `FIXED_INCOME_ATTRIBUTION_SUPPRESSED_REASON`
unconditionally for any held curated ticker, before any existing-entry branching,
so the leaf never reaches its no-profile branch for them; mutual funds are handled
by the leaf's own branch. One benign edge: a `fixed_income`-classified fund *not*
on the curated list, with no cache entry, reports as pending — self-correcting,
since it genuinely will be fetched and then refused as `ineligible`.

## 0. Findings from the code

These are what make the rest credible. Two of them changed the design.

### 0.1 The two sector panels share one denominator

Both are `% of invested NAV` — `portfolio-health.ts:383` and
`etf-look-through.ts:965` are the identical `pctOf(marketValue, investedNav)`
call. What differs is **completeness, not scale**: the wrapper bars sum to ~100%,
the look-through bars sum to `sectorCoveragePct`, and the remainder sits in
`sectorResidual.sharePct` — a field **the leaf already computes and the UI never
renders** (type at `:252`, computed at ~`:974`).

So the panels aren't contradictory answers on incomparable scales. They're the
same scale at two resolutions: full mass at coarse resolution (wrapper, where
"Funds" is one opaque block) versus fine resolution at partial mass
(look-through). Unifying them is arithmetic, not reconciliation — draw the
attributed bars plus the residual and it closes to 100% by construction, with no
renormalization.

What the user experienced is worse than two denominators: two panels on the
*same* scale, one of which silently doesn't add up. That reads as broken math.

**The "57.6% vs 22.7%" confusion** is cross-*axis*, not cross-denominator:
`Funds (no look-through)` is a wrapper **sector** bucket; `22.7%` is the
look-through **name**-axis coverage. Labelled alike, typeset alike, two blocks
apart, not comparable.

### 0.2 Sector → constituent symbols is not derivable for fund-held mass

Direct positions: the symbol is available today (`pos.ticker` is in scope at
`etf-look-through.ts:642`, just not retained). In fact the wrapper basis already
exposes it — `sectorExposure[].constituents` carries tickers per bucket.

Fund-attributed mass: the symbols **do not exist**. Decision 7 (`:35-37`) takes
the sector axis from the fund's own *aggregate* reported allocation.
`fp.sectors` (`{sector, weight}`) and `fp.constituents` (`{ticker, weight}`) are
independent, unlinked profile fields — nothing joins "SPY contributed 2.4% to
Technology" to a ticker. Deriving it would reverse Decision 7, need
classifications for hundreds of unseen tickers, and yield a sector total that
disagrees with the fund's own reported allocation.

**This is why the sector drill-down is cut** (§6): we could only ever half-answer
the user's "what symbols are in what sector," and the holdings view answers the
underlying need better.

### 0.3 "Effective positions" is a label collision

Verified: `health-section.tsx:260` renders `label="Effective positions"` for the
wrapper point estimate (`1/HHI`); `:430` renders the same label for the
look-through interval. Same label, twice, one scroll, different bases, wildly
different values.

The user read it literally and asked for something else entirely — *"how much of
each stock I own"* — which inverse-HHI does not measure. A label that reliably
makes people expect a different quantity is a defective label, and the quantity
they want is outcome 2.

### 0.4 The holdings table has no account provenance

`LookThroughPositionInput` (`:182-188`) carries no `accounts`.
`portfolio-health.ts:397` builds it from `[...merged.values()]`, already
ticker-merged, so **"how much" is already correct across accounts** — but
`m.accounts`, in scope on that very line, is dropped at the leaf boundary.
`EffectiveNamePosition.sources` carries `{from, marketValue}` only.

Net: the table answers *how much* ✓ and *through which wrapper* ✓, never *in
which account* ✗. That gap is half of outcome 2.

### 0.5 Decision 2, revised for the sector axis

Decision 2 kept the wrapper basis untouched with look-through strictly additive.
This design revises it **for the sector axis only**: one sector panel, on the
look-through basis.

Decision 2 protected the honesty invariants by *physical separation* — but
separation was a proxy for the requirement, not the requirement. The unified
panel enforces them structurally: the residual is a bar on the axis, so the axis
can't be read without reading it. That's stronger than a separate panel a reader
scrolls past. Wrapper-basis fields, the sizing gates, and the two-state
`lookThrough` contract are all unchanged.

## 1. Principles

**P1 — One question, one axis, one denominator.** One sector axis, `% of invested
NAV`, summing to 100%. The name axis and the sector axis never share a panel,
meter, or stat row.

**P2 — The unknown is a bar, never a gap.** Uncovered mass renders at true size on
the same scale. Never renormalized, never spread pro-rata across sectors. Every
sector bar therefore reads *"at least"* — the residual beside it is the
disclaimer.

**P3 — "Can't" and "haven't yet" look different.** Permanently excluded mass
(bond ETFs, mutual funds, leveraged, fund-of-funds) versus mass pending a fetch.
This is the difference between *stop waiting* and *come back later*, and it's the
only reason-detail the view owes the user.

**P4 — Diagnostic, never a gate.** Look-through doesn't move sizing. No warning
colours for low coverage — incompleteness is not a fault condition.

## 2. Outcome 1 — the unified sector panel

### 2.1 Structure

Attributed sector bars, then a terminal residual bar split two ways, on one axis
summing to 100% of invested NAV.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ SECTOR EXPOSURE — % OF INVESTED NAV                                       │
│ A lower bound. Uncovered fund weight is shown below as a residual,        │
│ never redistributed across sectors.                                       │
│                                                                           │
│  Sectors seen inside  ████████████████░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░   37.4%   │
│  $561k of $1.50M invested · $600k won't resolve · $339k pending           │
│                                                                           │
│  Technology     ████████████████████████████████████        14.2%        │
│  Financials     ████████████████████                         8.9%        │
│  Health care    ██████████████                               6.1%        │
│  Industrials    █████████                                    4.0%        │
│  Energy         ████                                         1.8%        │
│  Consumer disc. ███                                          1.4%        │
│  Unclassified   ██                                           1.0%        │
│  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  │
│  Not attributed ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒   62.6%   ▾   │
│                 └─── won't resolve ───┘└───── pending ─────┘             │
│                        40.0% $600k          22.6% $339k                  │
│                                                                           │
│  This read does not change position sizing in analysis runs.              │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Reading order: **how complete → what we know → what we don't → what it isn't
for.** The lower-bound sentence sits above every number it qualifies; the scope
note stays at the bottom. Splitting today's single trailing paragraph by function
is the whole fix for it.

**The meter reads `sectorCoveragePct`, not `coveragePct`.** An earlier draft put
the name-axis figure (22.7%) above sector bars totalling 37.4% and was caught in
review — reproducing the exact cross-axis confusion §0.1 exists to eliminate. If
the two axes ever share a panel again, that is the bug.

The residual bar is pinned last regardless of size — at 62.6% it would otherwise
sort to the top and read as the leading exposure rather than the boundary of
knowledge.

### 2.2 The residual, expanded

```
│    │ ◇ Won't resolve                                  40.0%    $600k   │ │
│    │   Never decompose by design (18.0%, $270k)                        │ │
│    │     BNDX GBIL LQD SCHP SHY SHYG TLT VCSH VWOB · VTSAX VFIAX · TQQQ│ │
│    │   Non-attributable rows inside funds we did read (22.0%, $330k)   │ │
│    │     Foreign lines, futures and cash.                              │ │
│    │   Coverage will not exceed 82.0%.                                 │ │
│    │                                                                   │ │
│    │ ○ Pending                                        22.6%    $339k   │ │
│    │   IEFA IEMG ACWX · VXUS SCHF                                      │ │
│    │   5 fund profiles fetch per visit — reload to see more.           │ │
```

**Two groups, not four.** The only question the user has is *"will this
improve?"*, so that is the only split. Data-quality refusals fold into **Pending**
(a thin or malformed profile can heal on the next 30-day refresh); the
non-attributable remainder inside covered funds folds into **Won't resolve**
(those rows are structurally unattributable). The specific per-fund reason string
stays available as a row tooltip — it just doesn't get its own top-level group.

The sub-lines exist because the groups must close to their bar
(18.0 + 22.0 = 40.0; 12.4 + 10.2 = 22.6), and because "coverage will not exceed
82.0%" — derived from the policy funds alone — is the single most useful sentence
in the panel for a user wondering whether to keep reloading.

Sector bars carry **no expand affordance**. See §6.

### 2.3 Worked-example key

All wireframe numbers come from one illustrative book (not the user's actual
figures). Invested NAV **$1,500,000**.

| Axis | Figure | % | $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sector** | attributed (`sectorCoveragePct`) | 37.4% | $561,000 |
| | residual — won't resolve | 40.0% | $600,000 |
| | residual — pending | 22.6% | $339,000 |
| **Name** | attributed (`coveragePct`) | 22.6% | $339,000 |
| | residual | 77.4% | $1,161,000 |

Both axes close to 100% independently. The attributed figures differ (37.4% vs
22.6%) because they are **different measurements** — a fund can clear the
coverage floor for sectors and fail it for names, or the reverse. That divergence
is correct and expected, and is exactly why the two axes never share a meter.

## 3. Outcome 2 — the holdings view

**This is the canonical answer to "how much do I own, and where."** Not the
sector panel, and not a diversification statistic.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ EFFECTIVE HOLDINGS — across every wrapper and account                     │
│ Largest: NVDA at least 7.4%          Name coverage 22.6% · $339k          │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Ticker            Effective weight     Value      Where                   │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ▾ NVDA                     7.4%      $111,000     Direct + VTI, QQQ       │
│     Direct · Taxable       3.1%       $46,500                             │
│     via VTI · Taxable      3.7%       $55,500                             │
│     via QQQ · Roth IRA     0.6%        $9,000                             │
│ ▸ AAPL                     3.1%       $46,500     Direct + 4              │
│ ▸ MSFT                     2.9%       $43,500     VTI, SPY, QQQ           │
│ ▸ AMZN                     2.2%       $33,000     VTI, SPY                │
│   …  (5 more rows)         2.0%       $30,000                             │
│ ▸ META                     0.6%        $9,000     VTI                     │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│   Top 10 shown            18.2%      $273,000                             │
│   + 214 smaller names      4.4%       $66,000                             │
│   Not attributed to a name 77.4%    $1,161,000    ▸ why                    │
│   Total                  100.0%     $1,500,000                            │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Four changes, in priority order:

1. **Residual + total footer.** The tail, the unattributed remainder, and a total
   reading exactly `100.0%`. The "broken math" perception dies here — the table
   closes. *Highest value-per-line change in this document*, and it needs no new
   data.
2. **Account provenance in the expansion.** One row per `(wrapper × account)`
   pair — `Direct · Taxable`, `via QQQ · Roth IRA`. This is the "and where" half
   of the ask, and the only part of outcome 2 needing a leaf change.
3. **Dollars alongside percents.** "How much do I own" is a dollar question first.
4. **`Where` column names sources, and every row expands.** Today `multi` gates
   both the chevron and the click handler (`:508-513`), so single-source rows are
   inert under rows that aren't.

Closure check: 18.2 + 4.4 + 77.4 = 100.0%; $273,000 + $66,000 + $1,161,000 =
$1,500,000. NVDA's sources sum to its row (3.1 + 3.7 + 0.6 = 7.4%).

**`Effective positions` is removed** from this block's stat row (§0.3). It stays
in the leaf and the analysis prompt, where a model can use the interval's width.

**Account-provenance data.** `LookThroughPositionInput` gains `accounts[]` (the
caller already holds it at `portfolio-health.ts:397`), and
`EffectiveNamePosition.sources[]` gains `accountId` / `accountLabel`. Direct
holdings split by their own per-account values; a fund slice splits pro-rata
across the accounts holding *that fund* — **exact, not an estimate** (SPY 60/40
across two accounts means SPY's Apple slice is 60/40). This belongs in the leaf
rather than the component: it's pro-rata money math over two joined datasets, and
doing it in the view would duplicate the leaf's fund/not-fund judgment.

## 4. States

The panel **always renders**. Today it's gated on `lookThrough === "partial"`
(`:303`), so at 0% coverage the user sees the old Funds bucket with no
explanation — the worst state getting the least information.

| State | Meter | Caption | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| No funds held | 100% | *(none)* | silent |
| 0% attributed | 0% | `$0 of $1.50M sectors seen inside · 5 profiles fetch per visit — reload to improve` | informational |
| Partial (typical) | 37.4% | `$561k of $1.50M sectors seen inside · $600k won't resolve · $339k pending` | informational |
| Ceiling reached | 82.0% | `Fully attributed except $270k in funds that never decompose by design` | **complete, not pending** |

Two rules this encodes: **the ceiling state changes register** — once only
permanently-excluded funds remain, every "reload" affordance disappears and the
language turns terminal, because implying progress after progress has stopped is
how an honest partial-coverage UI becomes a dishonest one. And **no state uses
warning colour** (P4).

### 4.1 The 0% state needs a leaf contract change

Ungating the panel is not sufficient. `summarizePortfolioHealth` discards exactly
the data the 0% view is made of:

```ts
lookThrough = anyFundAttributed ? "partial" : "none";
if (lookThrough === "none") lookThroughExposure = null;   // portfolio-health.ts:416
```

A book holding 44 funds with none yet attributed receives `null` — no
`sectorResidual`, no `opaqueFunds`. Rendering unconditionally would render empty.

**This is not new scope.** It's the twice-deferred "all-opaque diagnostics dropped
in the `none` state" follow-up (review rounds 4 and 24), deferred both times
because it touches a converged contract. This design is the first consumer that
actually needs it.

**Recommended: an additive sibling field, not a contract flip.**
`PortfolioHealth.lookThroughDiagnostics` carrying
`{ residual, sectorResidual, opaqueFunds } | null`, populated whenever an
`etfProfiles` map was supplied — including when nothing was attributed.
`lookThroughExposure` keeps its exact current meaning, so no consumer migrates.
Deleting the null-out is the smaller diff but the larger blast radius; take it
only if review prefers it and every consumer is re-checked. **Lands in Phase 2** —
it's a `portfolio-health.ts` change with tests, not a render tweak.

## 5. Visual encoding

The surface is a terminal-ish dark UI on OKLCH tokens
([`app/globals.css`](../../labs/trading-desk/app/globals.css)): 10–11.5px type, mono figures,
`tracking-wider` uppercase titles, 12px (`h-3`) `rounded-sm` bars on
`--c-surface-2` tracks. Keep all of it — conformance beats taste.

| Role | Token |
|---|---|
| Sector bar, meter fill | `--c-accent` — one hue for every bar |
| Residual — won't resolve | `--c-fg-faint` |
| Residual — pending | `--c-fg-faint` at ~55% opacity |
| Concentration alert chip | `--c-warn` + `⚠` (unchanged) |
| All text | `--c-fg` / `--c-fg-muted` / `--c-fg-faint` — never the data colour |

Sectors are **nominal categorical** — swapping their order changes nothing — so
every bar takes the same hue and no legend is needed. Do not colour bars by value
or give each sector its own hue: there are more than eight GICS sectors, and
value-colouring double-encodes what bar length already shows.

Two deliberate departures: **the meter never ramps to a warning colour** at low
fill (low coverage is incompleteness, not severity — P4), and **no texture on the
residual** (texture is reserved for `forced-colors`/print; the label does the
work). The residual's two segments are separated by a **2px gap in the surface
colour**, not a border. No gridlines — every bar is directly labelled.

Before ship, check `--c-fg-faint` at 55% opacity and `--c-warn` against
`--c-surface` at 3:1, both themes, with a contrast validator rather than by eye.

## 6. Deliberately not building

Each of these was designed in full and cut. They are defensible, not necessary.

| Cut | Why |
|---|---|
| **Sector → symbol drill-down** (+ a `contributors` leaf field) | §0.2 — only half-answerable: fund-held mass resolves to *"via VTI 4.8%"*, never to symbols. Not worth a new leaf field plus a disclosure layer when §3 answers the underlying need ("how much NVDA do I own") completely. **Cheap to revisit:** the direct half already exists in `sectorExposure[].constituents` with no leaf change. |
| **Basis toggle / statement view** | Two panels is the original defect; a mode switch just hides one behind a click. The consolidated fund list and statement read are preserved by giving `TopPositions` the same truncation footer (§7 Phase 1) — it already shows funds with per-account expand on the observed basis. |
| **Four-way residual taxonomy** | Collapsed to two (§2.2). The user's question is "will this improve?" — a third and fourth group answer a question nobody asked. |
| **Composition mini-bar** (direct vs via-funds per row) | Redundant with the expansion rows, which state the same split in numbers. |
| **`Effective positions`** | §0.3. Retained in the leaf and the analysis prompt. |
| **Renormalizing anything** | The methodology's central prohibition — a badly-covered fund's 7% constituent becomes a reported 27%. |
| **Look-through moving sizing gates** | A real-money change with its own sign-off. |

## 7. Plan

### Phase 1 — presentation only · no leaf change · ~half a day

`health-section.tsx` only. Ships independently and fixes most of the
perceived-correctness problem on its own.

1. Holdings table: residual + tail + total footer; dollars column; `Where` column
   names sources; every row expands.
2. `TopPositions`: same truncation footer + "show all" (`:596` has the identical
   `.slice(0, 10)` defect, and this is what preserves the statement-basis read).
3. Opaque funds regrouped **won't resolve / pending**, collapsible.
4. Lower-bound sentence moved above the numbers; scope note stays below.
5. `Effective positions` removed from the stat row.

(3) classifies by identity against the exported reason constants — not substring
matching — except for the two parameterized reasons, which need a prefix match.
**Mark that interim**; Phase 2's `reasonClass` replaces it. Depends on #927
(Baseline); items 1, 2, 4, 5 do not.

### Phase 2 — four additive fields, then unify · ~3 days

| Field | For |
|---|---|
| `LookThroughPositionInput.accounts[]` → `EffectiveNamePosition.sources[].accountId/accountLabel` | §3 — account provenance |
| `OpaqueFund.reasonClass: "permanent" \| "pending"` | §2.2 — replaces Phase 1's string matching |
| `OpaqueFund.marketValue` / `sharePct` | §2.2 — sizes the residual split (`mv` is already in scope at every site) |
| `PortfolioHealth.lookThroughDiagnostics` | §4.1 — makes the 0% state renderable; closes the twice-deferred follow-up |

Then: one `SectorPanel` (meter → bars → two-way residual → footnote) replacing
both `SectorExposure` and `LookThroughSectors`; panel renders unconditionally;
account-provenance rows in the holdings expansion. `FUNDS_BUCKET` stays a computed
leaf field for the analysis seed, just unrendered.

`appendLookThroughLines` ([`format.ts`](../../labs/trading-desk/flows/analysis/lib/format.ts))
changes in the same set — if the UI adopts two-way residual grouping and
mass-weighted coverage while the prompt block doesn't, the two surfaces describe
one methodology two ways, which is the failure this redesign exists to end.

All leaf changes are additive; existing consumers read named fields and none
breaks. Nothing is persisted, so no stored-shape compatibility is engaged.

**Do not add these rounds to the current PR.** File a follow-up issue and land on
its own branch with its own tests.

## 8. Settled decisions

| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Effective positions` is cut from the UI | Verified label collision (`:260` / `:430`); the user read it literally and wanted per-symbol ownership, which inverse-HHI doesn't measure. Kept in the leaf and prompt. |
| 2 | The holdings table is the canonical "how much do I own, and where" | The only surface aggregating one symbol across every wrapper and account. |
| 3 | No basis toggle — one unified sector panel | The two real losses are preserved by completing `TopPositions` instead of adding a mode. |
| 4 | `format.ts`'s prompt block changes in step | One methodology, described one way, on both surfaces. |
| 5 | Coverage expressed in NAV terms, not fund counts | *"I don't care how many funds it is in, I want to know how much I own and where."* |
| 6 | Decision 2 revised for the sector axis only | The unified panel enforces the honesty invariants structurally rather than by separation (§0.5). |
| 7 | Phase 2 is a follow-up issue, not more rounds on the current PR | The leaf merged after 46 rounds. |
| 8 | The name and sector axes never share a panel, meter, or stat row | The original defect was two axes typeset as comparable (§0.1). |
| 9 | The 0% view is enabled by an additive `lookThroughDiagnostics` field, not by flipping the null-out | Preserves the invariant every consumer relies on (§4.1). |
| 10 | Sector → symbol drill-down is deferred, not designed-in | Only half-answerable (§0.2); the holdings view serves the underlying need. |
