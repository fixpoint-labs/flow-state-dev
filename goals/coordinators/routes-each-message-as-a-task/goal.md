# coordinators › it routes each message as a task

**Issue:** FIX-2083 (epic FIX-2056). Its spec's goal: `specs/issues/FIX-2083/SPEC.md` → "The goal, and how we'll know it's met".

**Outcome:** a coordinator hands each of Alice's messages to each delegate it picks by filing a routed task: an ordinary task in Alice's list, run in the delegate's one session for the conversation, its result to the conversation, one attempt. Each answer lands once, under the delegate's name, and only after the work the delegate filed for it has ended. A second message to a busy delegate waits its turn. A round closes at its deadline, after a host restart too. A post row stored before the upgrade is worked as a routed task. Nothing writes or works a post.

**Retires** `goals/coordinators/one-fence-answers-each-post-once/` (FIX-1872). Its intent is kept here: each answer lands once however often its ending is replayed (**a:once**, five board touches while the endings are owed), one message filed for one delegate twice is one task (**a:twice**), a cancelled task's late answer lands nothing (**a:cancel**), a failed delegate's round closes at once (**b:failed**), and a late one's at its deadline, its answer landing once and going no further (**b:late**).

**Input:** a tree written at run time: four delegates on a goal-local `helper` flow, their ids and order picked at run time; a `writer` and a `lead` on the built-in `agent` flow, each with the first of them as its one delegate; eleven coordinators over them, `everyone`, `judgment`, `everyone` with `rounds: 1`, and `best-fit` with a fallback, each named in `host.mts`; and an app flow (`lab`) where Alice creates a project. A judgment coordinator opens workstreams with `openWorkstreamTool`, its lead checked (`leadFit`) on the same model best fit routes by. The real engine over a SQLite file with durable execution (so it keeps wakes), its HTTP router, driven through the shipped clients as Alice, in its own process (`host.mts`), with the round deadline at 2 s. Scripted, no model: a delegate answers `<worker> answers: <what it was handed>`, holds its answer on `[slow:<worker>]` (3 s) and `[slower:<worker>]` (20 s) and fails on `[fail:<worker>]`; the judgment turn files a `[judge]` message for the first delegate twice with `createTask`, a goal of its own and no `maxAttempts`, and a `[judge-3]` one with `maxAttempts: 3`; the writer files a piece for the first delegate, ends its turn, and answers the piece's ending with what the piece came back with; one scripted evaluation answers every fit judgment, best fit's and the lead vet's, recording each call: best fit's by a `[fit:<worker>]` mark (no mark fails the call), a lead's with *none fits* for research and *the work doesn't say its sort* for a bare name. Every word is picked at run time.

**Signal:** each failure is tagged `<leg>:<assertion>`.

- **a**: `a:tasks` one message to `everyone` is one task per delegate in Alice's list (`listTasks` through the public task action), each read through `getTask` with session `ongoing`, result to `conversation` and one attempt, worked in its delegate's session and answered; `a:once` five touches of the board, fired as soon as the first ending is written and while the rest are owed, still leave one line per delegate's answer, under its name; `a:judgment-attempts` the judgment turn's `createTask`, with `maxAttempts` left out, reads one attempt; `a:three-refused` its `maxAttempts: 3` is refused at filing, naming FIX-2080's rule (`conversation_result_one_attempt`), and files nothing; `a:twice` its filing one message for one delegate twice is one task, recorded delivered then skipped, with one line; `a:waits` a second message to a delegate busy on the first waits pending in Alice's list and starts once the first has ended, in the same session; `a:cancel-waiting` Alice's cancel of a third message's task while it waits ends it cancelled, never started; `a:cancel` Alice's cancel of a task while its delegate holds its answer ends it cancelled and closes the round then, before its deadline; when the delegate finishes, its answer lands no line.
- **b**: `b:restart` the host is killed (its whole process group, SIGKILL) while one delegate works far past the round's deadline and the other has answered, and started again on the same store: the round closes within 2 s of the later of its deadline and the restart; `b:failed` a delegate that fails lands one line under its name, `<worker> couldn't answer: <its error>` (FIX-1872 BR-9 as amended, FIX-2266), and nothing that answers the message; its task ends errored, and its round closes as soon as both delegates have ended, before the deadline; `b:late` a delegate still working at the deadline sees its round close then (within 2 s), and its answer lands once when it comes and is handed to nobody in round 1, which carries only the other delegate's answer.
- **c**: `c:after` the writer's routed task, whose turn filed a piece and ended, lands one line under the writer's name, carrying the piece's result, and ends after the piece has.
- **d**: `d:word` the delegate the judgment turn filed for heard Alice's message as she wrote it, held-out word and all, with the turn's own goal beside it. The one fit check (D3), each read from the scripted evaluation's own record: `d:fit-pick` best fit's message marked for a delegate is judged by one fit check call offering both delegates, placed by its pick (`by: evaluated`) and heard by that delegate alone; `d:fit-fallback` an unmarked message is judged by one call, which fails, and the fallback delegate takes it (`by: fallback`, `fit.reason: failed`); `d:lead-refused` a worker's open of research under the lead is judged by one call, asked about the lead with two more choices (three in all), and refused `lead-doesnt-fit` on its *none fits*, and nothing opens; `d:lead-bare` an open of a bare name is judged by one call and opens, `leadFit: { checked: true }`; `d:one-check` no language model is asked to judge fit on the fit model but the check's own ask again after a failed evaluation.
- **e**: `e:legacy` a post row seeded in the store as a coordinator before FIX-2083 left it (pending, `sealed.kind: "post"`, no session or result setting) is worked once, as a routed task, in the delegate's session for the conversation, and its answer lands once under its name.
- **sweep**: `sweep:entry` no registered flow declares a post entry (`onDelegatedPost`); `sweep:rows` no post row is written in the run; `sweep:field` no tool the judgment turn is offered has a `post` field.

**Not here:** leg d's workstream half (a workstream ask lands in the workstream's session) is graded by the FIX-2005 hand-off leg of `goals/workforce-privacy/two-users-share-a-project-and-nothing-else/` (b3), on a real model, and by Shift Manager's `devteam-em-workstream` and `project-workstreams` tests.

**Anti-game:** the check writes no row, ending or session but leg e's seed, which stands for the store an upgrade finds: every change goes through an action, as Alice, through the router. Task reads are the public task actions (`listTasks_tasks`, `getTask_tasks`) as Alice, answers read back from the session's request list as an app does. The rows and the conversations' records are read from the SQLite file, read-only, from the rows Alice owns: no route sends a client a board's rows with their server-written brief. The lines, routing records and tool results are read through the session route as Alice. Who heard what, in which session and when, is the scripted delegate's own record. Rounds and their deadlines are read at the moment they matter from the engine's session store, read-only. The kill reaches the process `tsx` starts, so nothing the first host held in memory (a timer) survives it. The engine's request store is read only to wait until nothing runs.

**Controls:**

- `GOAL_CONTROL=round-timer` keeps a round's deadline on an in-process timer, never a wake (the coordinator reads the host as keeping none). It must FAIL `b:restart`: the timer dies with the host. `b:late`, on a live host, stays green.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=post-map-off` turns FIX-2080's map entry `post` off, so a stored post row reads as the row it is. It must FAIL `e:legacy`.
- `GOAL_COMMIT=b43150fea` (`main` before FIX-2083) must FAIL `c:after` and the sweep.
- `GOAL_COMMIT=e196901d5` (`main` before D3, with its two fit calls) must FAIL `d:lead-refused` and `d:one-check`: its lead vet asks a generator of its own, not the fit check.

**Run:**

```
pnpm tsx goals/coordinators/routes-each-message-as-a-task/run.mts
GOAL_CONTROL=round-timer pnpm tsx goals/coordinators/routes-each-message-as-a-task/run.mts
GOAL_CONTROL=post-map-off pnpm tsx goals/coordinators/routes-each-message-as-a-task/run.mts
GOAL_COMMIT=<sha> pnpm tsx goals/coordinators/routes-each-message-as-a-task/run.mts
```
