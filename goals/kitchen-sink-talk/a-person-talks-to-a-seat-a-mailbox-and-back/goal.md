# kitchen-sink-talk › a person talks to a seat, a mailbox, and back

**Issue:** FIX-1601, the closure of the epic FIX-1592. Legs a to c are the epic's goal (ER-17), `seg` is part 4's segmentation row. The plan is `specs/issues/FIX-1601/PLAN.md`, which owns the leg definitions. Rewritten by FIX-1792 P1; the folder name is kept so its verdict history stays in one place.

**Outcome:** A person asks `support.help`, the support desk's coordinator, a question and, without reloading, sees the one specialist whose purpose fits start work and then answer under its own name. Three questions reach three specialists, one each, and each specialist keeps only its own cases. A direct conversation with `support.devices` remembers its first message, stays out of the conversation with `support.help`, and is still there after a reload. The gated legs run keyless; a real-model smoke gets one answer per post.

**Rewritten (FIX-1792 P1).** `support.help` is a coordinator worker file on `routing: best-fit`, no longer a mailbox, and the escalation feature is removed, not replaced (FIX-1792 BR-14; product owner, 2026-10-06). What changed, and why:

- **e** (part 2: a case filed onto `escalations`, `e:line`, `e:row-open`, `e:row`, `e:warning`) and the `no-filing` control go with the escalation feature. Nothing files a case.
- A specialist's posts are no longer its runs "in the mailbox": each is the specialist's own session for the person's conversation with `support.help` (a session on the `agent` flow, listed in the rail as a run of the conversation). **b:only-its-own** and **seg** read those. Since FIX-1788 the rail has no row per specialist, so which sessions are a specialist's is read from the server by the session's `workerId`, as an index; what each holds is read off the page. **c1** starts its conversation with "Talk" in the roster panel rather than "New conversation" on a rail row.
- `c2:mailbox` is renamed `c2:coordinator`: the conversation with `support.help` shows nothing of the direct talk.
- The `main` control and `fixtures/today-main.json` go: the app it ran (before FIX-1609 and FIX-1610) has no coordinator to post to, so this check cannot run there.
- `no-landing` now takes every answer away, not only a text answer that skipped the post tool: a delegate's answer is its turn's reply, handed back to the coordinator, and the control's flow hands nothing back. It names **b:line** beside **a:line**.
- The smoke's follow-up ("It sees it. It fails right after the password.") is no longer pinned to its antecedent's specialist, and its answer is not graded on naming the subject. Best fit's one evaluation is handed the post alone, not the lines before it, so a follow-up is routed on its own words. This is the same finding as `workforce-mailboxes/a-routed-post-gets-one-answer`'s retired **followup** and **context** legs, put to the product owner on FIX-1792's P1 PR.

**Input:** `fixtures/input.json`: the coordinator and the person's label; the four specialists by role and the fallback; the scripted markers (`[route:<seat>]`, the text answer `[scenario:wake-after-a-hold]` and the post answer `[scenario:reply-after-a-hold]`, each of which holds a few seconds before it answers so a working row has time to show, `[scenario:talk-to-seat]`, `[scenario:recall]`) and the reply markers each writes; how long a line may take (15 s); the smoke's posts, which are FIX-1610's `poc/route-choice` posts plus the wifi line's follow-up. Held-out: every post carries a fresh token shaped `/[a-z]+-token-[a-z0-9]+/` (what the recall scenario names), and only this run's tokens are graded, so a line another run left behind can never be the one that passes.

**Signal:** one real browser against kitchen-sink's **production build**, built once by the run, served by `next start` on the scripted model, the in-memory store and **no model key**. The page is loaded once per journey. Each leg reads the **open page** first and reloads once, at its end, and reads again.

- **a** (P1a). Post `[route:support.devices] [scenario:wake-after-a-hold]` and token A. Graded as **a:working**, the panel shows `support.devices is working` in a reading before the one that first shows its line; **a:line**, within 15 s of Send exactly one line answers the post, by `support.devices`, carrying `[reply:wake]`; **a:clears**, the row is gone within 5 s of that line; **a:alone**, no other seat shows as working; **a:kept**, after the reload the post and its one line are still there.
- **b** (P1b). Three posts carrying `[scenario:reply-after-a-hold]`, each after the previous answer shows and its row clears: B1 routed to `support.devices`, B2 to `support.accounts`, B3 with no route marker, for the fallback `support.general`. **b:working**, for each post the panel shows its seat working in a reading before the one that first shows its line; **b:line**, each gets exactly one line carrying its token and `[reply:in-mailbox]`, by its seat, within 15 s; **b:clears**, each post's row is gone within 5 s of its line (B3's too), and no row still shows when the next post would go; **b:alone**, no other seat shows as working, counting a row left from an earlier post; **b:only-its-own**, read from each specialist's session for the conversation, on the open page and after the reload: `devices` holds A and B1, `accounts` B2, `general` B3, each heard once with a reply under it, `fsd` holds nothing, and nobody holds another's token; **b:kept**, after the reload each post and its line show once.
- **c1** (P1c). "Talk" on `support.devices` in the roster, `[scenario:talk-to-seat]` and token C1: **c1:turn**, C1 is the person's turn, once; **c1:reply**, `[reply:talk-to-seat]` under it; on the open page and after the reload, reopened from the rail.
- **c2** (P1c). In that conversation, `[scenario:recall]` and token C2: **c2:turn**, C2 is the person's turn, once; **c2:recall**, the reply under it carries `[reply:recall]`, names C1, and names none of A, B1 to B3. **c2:coordinator**: the conversation with `support.help` shows no line carrying C1, C2 or either reply marker. Both on the open page and after the reload.
- **seg** (part 4). After leg c, `support.devices`' session for the conversation with `support.help` holds nothing of C1 or C2.

**Anti-game:** no reload before an assertion, and none but the one at each leg's end. Nothing is graded on wording past a script-written marker, a return value, a server read or a CLI run: the server is read only as an index (which sessions are a specialist's, which one "Talk" opened) and to wait. Only this run's tokens count. A specialist's session is read only once the rail marks it current and the panel draws that session's own messages (by the server's count and text, a wait that is not graded): the panel keeps the last session's items for a moment, and a seat panel draws a coordinator conversation's messages as turns too. Leg c's reads are the conversation log (`[data-message-role]`), never the composer.

**Model:** n/a for the gated legs: kitchen-sink's scripted model scripts best fit's evaluation and answers (epic FIX-1592 D3). The run refuses to start when a provider key is set, and the server runs with `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY` empty. The smoke alone has a key: the app's own models, its best fit on `vercel/typesafe-ai/jev`.

**Run:** `PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers pnpm tsx goals/kitchen-sink-talk/a-person-talks-to-a-seat-a-mailbox-and-back/run.mts`, with no provider key in the environment. It takes the plain journey, then every control below, each on a fresh `next start` and a fresh browser context, on the one build. PASS needs the plain journey green and every control behaving.

**Smoke:** `GOAL_LIVE=1` on the same command, with `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY`: the smoke alone, out of test mode. Six posts to `support.help`, each after the previous answer and every run it started are done: a laptop that won't join the wifi, its follow-up "It sees it. It fails right after the password.", then the device, account, FSD and unclear posts. Each gets exactly one answer line within two minutes on the open page, by a specialist, the one the fixture names where it names one, and no other seat shows as working. A post with no answer, or with a run still working two minutes after its answer, fails the smoke, and no later post is sent, so each answer counted is its own post's. After a reload each post sent still has its one line. Never retried: a missing answer is a finding (QR-8a).

**Controls:** a control passes only when it reddens every assertion its row names (its own signal), fails no leg at setup, and leaves each leg it keeps green wholly green. Another red assertion in a leg its signal sits in is reported, not counted; a leg in neither list is reported and not graded. A control reddens an assertion only where the plain journey passed it on the same build: one red in both is the build's and is reported as inherited, and a named one that is inherited fails the control.

| Control | Where it acts | Must FAIL, at each of these | Stays green |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GOAL_CONTROL=no-live` (FIX-1609) | the page, opened with `?goalControl=no-live`: the conversation's live view | `a:line`, `b:line` (no answer until the reload; not the working rows, which the page can still draw from the read it makes when the person posts, depending on which read wins) | c1, c2, seg |
| `GOAL_CONTROL=no-landing` (kitchen-sink's `lib/landing-control.ts`) | the server | `a:line`, `b:line` (a specialist answers in its own session and hands nothing back) | c1, c2, seg |
| `GOAL_CONTROL=no-route` (kitchen-sink's `lib/coordinator-control.ts`) | the server | `a:line`, `a:alone`, `b:line`, `b:alone` (best fit read as `everyone`: every specialist works and answers) | c1, c2, seg |
| `GOAL_CONTROL=drop-user-message` (FIX-1585) | this run, at the page's `fetch`: leg c's snapshot, its action streams and its live session stream lose their `user` items | `c1:turn`, `c2:turn` | a, b, seg |
| `GOAL_CONTROL=no-history` (FIX-1612, switch from FIX-1611) | the server | `c2:recall` | a, b, c1, seg |

`drop-user-message` filters at the page's `fetch` rather than with Playwright's routing because the live session stream never ends, and a routed response is read whole before the page gets any of it.
