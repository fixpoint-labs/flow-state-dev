# kitchen-sink-talk › a person talks to a seat, a channel, and back

**Issue:** FIX-1601, the closure of the epic FIX-1592. Legs a to c are the epic's goal (ER-17), the escalation is part 2 (ER-25), `seg` is part 4's segmentation row. The plan is `specs/issues/FIX-1601/PLAN.md`, which owns the leg definitions.

**Outcome:** A person asks `support.help` a question and, without reloading, sees the one specialist whose purpose fits start work and then answer under its own name. Three questions reach three specialists, one each, and each specialist keeps only its own cases. A direct conversation with `support.devices` remembers its first message, stays out of the channel, and is still there after a reload. A case that needs a person is filed onto `escalations`, and the specialist says so. The gated legs run keyless; a real-model smoke gets one answer per post.

**Input:** `fixtures/input.json`: the channel, its board and the person's label; the four specialists by role, the fallback and the specialist part 2 routes to; the scripted markers (`[route:<seat>]`, the text answer `[scenario:wake-after-a-hold]` and the post-tool answer `[scenario:reply-after-a-hold]`, each of which holds a few seconds before it answers so a working row has time to show, `[scenario:talk-to-seat]`, `[scenario:recall]`, `[scenario:needs-a-person]`) and the reply markers each writes; how long a line may take (15 s); the smoke's posts, which are FIX-1610's `poc/route-choice` posts plus the wifi line its follow-up leans on. `fixtures/today-main.json` is the same with today's `main` roster, for the `main` control. Held-out: every post carries a fresh token shaped `/[a-z]+-token-[a-z0-9]+/` (what the recall scenario names), and only this run's tokens are graded, so a line another run left behind can never be the one that passes.

**Signal:** one real browser against kitchen-sink's **production build**, built once by the run, served by `next start` on the scripted model, the in-memory store and **no model key**. The page is loaded once per journey. Each leg reads the **open page** first and reloads once, at its end, and reads again.

- **a** (P1a). Post `[route:support.devices] [scenario:wake-after-a-hold]` and token A. Graded as **a:working**, the panel shows `support.devices is working` in a reading before the one that first shows its line; **a:line**, within 15 s of Send exactly one line answers the post, by `support.devices`, carrying `[reply:wake]`; **a:clears**, the row is gone within 5 s of that line; **a:alone**, no other seat shows as working; **a:kept**, after the reload the post and its one line are still there. The text answer never calls the post tool, so the line landed by FIX-1610's rule alone.
- **b** (P1b). Three posts carrying `[scenario:reply-after-a-hold]`, each after the previous answer shows and its row clears: B1 routed to `support.devices`, B2 to `support.accounts`, B3 with no route marker, for the fallback `support.general`. **b:working**, for each post the panel shows its seat working in a reading before the one that first shows its line; **b:line**, each gets exactly one line carrying its token and `[reply:in-channel]`, by its seat, within 15 s; **b:clears**, each post's row is gone within 5 s of its line (B3's too), and no row still shows when the next post would go; **b:alone**, no other seat shows as working, counting a row left from an earlier post; **b:only-its-own**, read from each specialist's conversation in the channel, on the open page and after the reload: `devices` holds A and B1, `accounts` B2, `general` B3, each heard once with a reply under it, `fsd` holds nothing, and nobody holds another's token; **b:kept**, after the reload each post and its line show once.
- **c1** (P1c). "New conversation" on `support.devices`, `[scenario:talk-to-seat]` and token C1: **c1:turn**, C1 is the person's turn, once; **c1:reply**, `[reply:talk-to-seat]` under it; on the open page and after the reload.
- **c2** (P1c). In that conversation, `[scenario:recall]` and token C2: **c2:turn**, C2 is the person's turn, once; **c2:recall**, the reply under it carries `[reply:recall]`, names C1, and names none of A, B1 to B3. **c2:channel**: `support.help` shows no line carrying C1, C2 or either reply marker. Both on the open page and after the reload.
- **seg** (part 4). After leg c, `support.devices`' conversation in `support.help` holds nothing of C1 or C2.
- **e** (P2e, part 2). Post `[route:support.accounts] [scenario:needs-a-person]` and token E. **e:line**, one line answers it by `support.accounts`, carrying `[reply:escalated]`, on the open page and after the reload; **e:row-open**, within 15 s of the reading that first showed that line (of when it was due, if none did) and with no reload, the team panel's `escalations` list shows exactly one row carrying E; **e:row**, the same after the reload; **e:warning**, the boot warns that `support.help.escalations` is unattended, and warns about no other board.

**Anti-game:** no reload before an assertion, and none but the one at each leg's end. Leg a's scenario never calls the post tool. Nothing is graded on wording past a script-written marker, a return value, a server read or a CLI run: the server is read only to know the row was written before part 2's reload. Only this run's tokens count. A seat's conversation is read only once the rail marks it current and the panel has replaced the last one. Leg c's reads are the conversation log (`[data-message-role]`), never the composer.

**Model:** n/a for the gated legs: kitchen-sink's scripted model routes and answers (epic FIX-1592 D3). The run refuses to start when a provider key is set, and the server runs with `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY` empty. The smoke alone has a key: the app's own models, its route on `vercel/typesafe-ai/jev`.

**Run:** `PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers pnpm tsx goals/kitchen-sink-talk/a-person-talks-to-a-seat-a-channel-and-back/run.mts`, with no provider key in the environment. It takes the plain journey, then every control below except `main`, each on a fresh `next start` and a fresh browser context, on the one build. PASS needs the plain journey green and every control behaving.

**Smoke:** `GOAL_LIVE=1` on the same command, with `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY`: the smoke alone, out of test mode. Six posts to `support.help`, each after the previous answer and every run it started are done: a laptop that won't join the wifi, its follow-up "It sees it. It fails right after the password.", then the device, account, FSD and unclear posts. Each gets exactly one answer line within two minutes on the open page, by the specialist the fixture names, and no other seat shows as working. The follow-up's answer is by its antecedent's specialist and names the subject (one of the fixture's words); the run prints both answers. A post with no answer, or with a run still working two minutes after its answer, fails the smoke, and no later post is sent, so each answer counted is its own post's. After a reload each post sent still has its one line. Never retried: a missing answer is a finding (QR-8a).

**Controls:** a control passes only when it reddens every assertion its row names (its own signal), fails no leg at setup, and leaves each leg it keeps green wholly green. Another red assertion in a leg its signal sits in is reported, not counted; a leg in neither list is reported and not graded. A control reddens an assertion only where the plain journey passed it on the same build: one red in both is the build's and is reported as inherited, and a named one that is inherited fails the control.
