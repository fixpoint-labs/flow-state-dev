# shift-manager › it groups workstreams under their projects

**Issue:** FIX-1718; rewritten for FIX-1793, where a project's Stream became each person's own project coordinator and its work became workstream entries, each with one owner, that everyone in the organization reads; converted for FIX-1792, where a project stopped listing mailboxes.

**Outcome:** A person opening Shift Manager on a Lab finds every shared project the Lab holds and their own private ones, each grouping its workstreams on its screen, then No project with any mailbox workstream. Each project's four tabs show that project as they read it: Stream is their own project coordinator for it, Workstreams lists every entry with the progress counted from them, Board draws their own workstream sessions, Brief its brief. A workstream someone opens from the app is read by everyone in the project, and only its owner writes it; a burst of writes from many owners lands whole and survives a restart. A person who asks the chief of staff for projects gets them, owned by them.

**Input:** the DevTeam profile (`packages/shift-manager/teams/devteam/fsdev.config.mts`), unedited, on a fresh store. At boot it creates its default projects through the project writes' own `createProject`, each with the member and the crowd as members, and opens two workstreams in one of them (Storefront's `feature` and `release`) as the member, each led by the EM. Verified users, all in one organization: the owner (the page's person); the members, the lab's member and its crowd of seven (`LAB_CROWD` in `packages/shift-manager/teams/devteam/host.mts`); and an outsider, on no project. Before the screens, the owner creates one private project through the projects flow's `createProject`, as an app does. The Lab is served with its checked store writes (a write that names the version it read) held for a random 0 to 30 ms (`packages/shift-manager/teams/devteam/write-latency.mts`, switched on by `DEVFORCE_LAB_WRITE_LATENCY_MS`, never in production code), so a read and its write are far enough apart for a burst to race. Every run uses it, the plain one and each control, and the run fails if the Lab's log doesn't say it holds them. The fixture (`fixtures/input.json`) holds the private project's title and brief, the titles of the two workstreams opened from the page, the race's workstream, its burst sizes and its changes, and the chief of staff's ask, and is held out: other text and other sizes must still pass. Project, workstream and worker names, the project coordinator and the workstream coordinator are read from the store, the tree and the Lab's `projectSetup` at run time. The `cos` leg asks as the member, so its rows can't be mistaken for the profile's (the owner's); the two titles it asks for carry the run's stamp, so no file names them.

**Signal:** each failure is tagged with its leg.

- **store** (precondition): the Lab holds at least one shared project row, each owned by the profile's person, and the owner reads a private project.
- **PROJECTS equals the store's rows:** for the owner, and for the member after the entries leg, the sidebar's project groups, in order, are the shared `projects` rows, then that person's own private rows, none listing a mailbox, then No project with every inventoried mailbox workstream (shown only when there is one; the DevTeam has none). The member's PROJECTS never lists the owner's private project.
- **the boot's workstreams:** a shared project holds at least two workstreams another member opened at boot, each with its session, and the owner's Workstreams tab lists them (graded against the store under the four tabs).
- **a project's four tabs:** for every project the person reads, shared and private: Brief draws the row's brief; Workstreams lists exactly the project's entries (by owner and id), its progress counts them and their objectives met, and it lists no mailbox; Board draws a lane for each of the person's own entries with a workstream session, by that session, and none of anyone else's; Stream draws a conversation naming a session that is the person's own, on the project coordinator's flow, naming the project coordinator as its worker and this project as its project. No two projects' Streams, and not the owner's and the member's Stream on one project, are one session. No tab draws a `project-*-empty` state or copy naming FIX-1650.
- **entries opened through the app show for both members:** the owner and the member, each in their own page, open a workstream from a shared project's Workstreams tab. The store then holds one entry for each, owned by its opener, naming a workstream session of the opener's and led by a worker on the opener's own roster. Both pages list both entries, with progress counting them (graded under the four tabs above), and each opens the other's entry on its details.
- **the outsider's open is refused:** the outsider reads the project's entries as the members do (everyone in the organization reads them), and its `openWorkstream` in the project ends refused; the project holds no entry of its.
- **a write race lands whole:** every member (the owner, the member and the crowd) opens a workstream with the same id in one shared project, each from several new sessions, all sent at once; then each sends several updates to their own entry at once, every field (title, status, due, objectives) changed by several of them, each from a session of its own. Two owners' entries are two keys and never meet; each owner's own opens and updates race on their one entry. Every open and update completes, all of an owner's opens answer one session, exactly one says it made the entry, the store holds one entry per owner naming that session, and every field holds what was sent.
- **cos** (needs a model): the member asks the chief of staff, in one line, for two projects and names the owner as a member of the first. The store then holds the two, titled as asked, each owned by the member, and no other new project. The first is a shared row listing the member and the owner as members. The second, asked for "just for me", is either the member's private project, read from their user scope through their conversation with the chief of staff, or a shared row with the member alone. A private project is what "just for me" asks for, so both readings pass.
- **a restart keeps the projects and their entries:** the Lab is stopped and started again on the same store. Every project the owner reads, shared and private, is as it was (title, brief, owner, members, repository), and every entry of each is as it was (title, lead, session, status, due, objectives, opened and updated times), none added.

**Anti-game:** expected values come from the store, the tree and the Lab's `projectSetup`, never from the check or from Shift Manager's own data module. The `cos` leg grades the store, never the chief of staff's words: without the tool, the model has answered that it created both projects while the store held none. The shared rows are written by the profile's own code at boot. The page's workstreams are opened through its own form, as a person opens one; the race and the outsider go through the projects flow's actions over HTTP, each with that user's own bearer; entries are read back over HTTP through each user's own roster session. The outsider runs in the same organization, so only the membership check stands between it and an open.

**Model:** `anthropic/claude-haiku-5-5` (the DevTeam chief of staff's own) for the chief of staff in the `cos` leg, through the default resolver; it needs a key that serves the model the chief of staff's file names (`keysServing` in `goals/lib/model.mts`; for an Anthropic model, `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY`), and the leg is blocked and says so when none is set. The other legs are model-free: opening a Stream starts its coordinator session and runs no turn.

**Model-free run:** `GOAL_LEG=model-free` (every leg but `cos`; `pnpm goal:all --model-free` runs it this way)

**Run:** `PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers pnpm tsx goals/shift-manager/it-groups-workstreams-under-their-projects/run.mts` runs every leg. `GOAL_LEG=model-free` runs all but `cos` (screens, entries, race and restart); `GOAL_LEG=cos` runs only `cos`. The `cos` leg runs last, because its rows are the member's and the other legs grade every shared row as the owner's.

**Controls:** the page controls rebuild Shift Manager with one source module swapped for one under `controls/`, as `it-opens-a-lab` does; the build fails if the swap never fired. The server controls load the Lab with a Node module hook (`controls/swap-hook.mjs`) that swaps one source module, and the run fails if no import was swapped.

- `GOAL_CONTROL=unread`: the grouping ignores the rows and lists every workstream on its own. Must fail at **PROJECTS equals the store's rows**. With no project grouped, every project's page is "No such project", so the four tabs and the entries leg fail with it.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=gap-tabs`: the project level as it was before projects, every tab a FIX-1650 empty state. Must fail at **a project's four tabs**; with no form to open a workstream from, the entries leg fails with it.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-gate`: `membership-gate.ts` answers yes for everyone. Must fail at **the outsider's open is refused**, and only there.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-tool`: the Lab's `host.mts` is swapped for one whose `openLab` leaves the project tools out of the catalog the chief of staff's turn is built with and out of every worker's `tools:`, as before the chief of staff had them. The Lab boots as before. Must fail at **cos**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=drop-solo`: the workforce's `project-writes.ts` is swapped for one whose `createProject` answers a create the asker holds alone, shared or private, as if it wrote it, and writes nothing. The first project and the profile's defaults name other members and are written. Must fail at **cos**, on the second project. Runs only with `GOAL_LEG=cos`: the owner's private project in the model-free legs is such a create.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-retry`: `cas-retry.ts` makes one attempt, so an entry's compare-and-swap writes get only the engine's own retries. Must fail at **a write race lands whole**, and only there. Four writers on one entry did not fail without it (the engine's three retries absorbed them), which is why each field is sent several times.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=in-memory`: store-sqlite's `index.ts` hands back in-memory stores, the profile as it was before its store was kept on disk. Must fail at **a restart keeps the projects and their entries**.
