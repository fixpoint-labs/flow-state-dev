# shift-manager › it opens a lab

**Issue:** FIX-1662. multi-seat-collab converted by FIX-1792 P3: it declares no mailbox, and its planner files on its own conversation's board. DevTeam's half rewritten under FIX-1792, which took the DevTeam off mailboxes; rewritten again for FIX-1792 P4, which removed Shift Manager's mailbox workstream level: a workstream is now an entry a person opens in a project.

**Outcome:** A person runs Shift Manager's start command over a Lab's own `fsdev.config.mts`, one it was never told about, and every screen they reach shows what that Lab holds. The sidebar lists the Lab's teams and workers. A workstream the person opens in a project shows its entry there, and, as its owner, they see the lead's board: the project Board's lane for that workstream's session draws the rows its lead keeps there, at their stored status. Tasks lists the rows that aren't done, each conversation's own board's, and Inbox lists what is waiting on them or says plainly that nothing is. A line they post appears once its session has kept it, and it is still there after a reload.

**Input:** the two goal Labs as they stand, DevTeam (bearer-authenticated, in-memory) and multi-seat-collab (SQLite, no auth). Their configs are not edited. The fixture (`fixtures/input.json`) holds the composer line, the DevTeam feature filed, and the multi-seat-collab piece of work. It is held out: other text, another issue slug, or another desk the Lab routes must still pass a correct Shift Manager. Every worker name is read from the trees at run time.

**Signal:** per Lab, each failure tagged `[<lab>] <leg>`:

- **store** (precondition): the Lab's inventory holds exactly the tree's workers, and a conversation's board holds at least one row.
- **TEAMS equals the store's seats**: the sidebar's TEAMS squares, one per seat, equal the inventory's seat rows, and each team's row has a square for exactly its seats (a seat with no team sits in the Staff row).
- **Tasks equals the store's open rows**: grouped by state, by worker and by stream, the rows are exactly the stored rows that are neither completed nor cancelled and not queued (pending, blocked or of an unknown status). With the Queued toggle on, they are every such row. The toggle's count equals the stored queued rows. Every row is on a conversation's own board (`<sessionId>.tasks`), and the task frame opened from each conversation's first row in Tasks titles the stored row.
- **Inbox equals the store's pending asks**: the count equals the stored suspensions with no resume, on seat-owned sessions, asking a person. With none pending, Inbox says nothing needs the person, with the store's count of running sessions.
- **reach**: every level, tab and panel opens (Shift Coordinator with its summary and panel, Inbox by its route, Tasks, and the task frame's four tabs and panel slot). Every empty one names what will be there. The page throws nothing.
- **the post appears on screen**: on DevTeam, a line sent from the opened workstream entry's composer, a turn to its lead, is drawn in that entry's conversation. In a Lab with no workstream (multi-seat-collab), the post goes through the task composer on an open conversation-board row, and the composer says it was delivered.
- **the post is in the stored transcript**: on DevTeam, the workstream's session holds exactly one user message with that body, and a reload draws it once. In a Lab with no workstream, the session the row names as its run holds exactly one user message with that body.
- **a workstream shows its entry** (DevTeam): the person opens a workstream in the organization's first project, led by the tree's EM, and the project's Workstreams tab lists it, by its owner and id.
- **its owner sees the lead's board** (DevTeam): the rows filed on that workstream session's board are drawn in the project Board's lane for that session, each at its stored status, and nothing else is.
- **an answer lands in the store**: when an ask is pending (DevTeam's EM raises one at boot, in its own session), Shift Manager offers Approve on at least one in Inbox, and Approve leaves that suspension, and only it, no longer pending in the store. Asks pending with an answer offered on none is a failure.
- **an answer leaves Inbox**: after that Approve, Inbox no longer lists the ask.

The rows come from the Lab's own doors. On multi-seat-collab, the planner files on its own conversation's board through `createTask_tasks`, for the delegate the app routes the piece's desk to, and the board hands the row to that worker, which holds it on its question (FIX-2131: a running row with an open question, listed in Inbox). On DevTeam, the person opens a workstream led by the EM through the projects flow's `openWorkstream`, as the app opens one, and files the feature on its session's board through the EM's `file` door; the board hands it to the coder at once, so it runs. A follow-up that waits on it is filed through the same board's `createTask_tasks`, and stays queued while the feature runs. DevTeam's post goes through that workstream page's composer, a turn to the EM in its session.

**Anti-game:** a hollow pass would be a Shift Manager that draws names it was built with, draws or reports a post before its session keeps it, or is graded against its own reads. So the check never asserts on Shift Manager's own data module. The oracles are the tree on disk (`readDeclaredRoster`) and the store, read through the Lab's HTTP routes by this script's own requests, with the bearer the page was handed. The store is read after the page's refresh, so a UI that invents rows or drops them fails. Each control proves one leg can go red.

**Model:** n/a (model-free: the DevTeam harness is the lab's scripted stub, and multi-seat-collab has no model)

**Run:** `PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers pnpm tsx goals/shift-manager/it-opens-a-lab/run.mts`

**Controls:** each control rebuilds Shift Manager with source modules swapped for a module under `controls/`. The build fails if a swap never fired.

- `GOAL_CONTROL=static-names`: the seat list is written in from the DevTeam tree. A written-in seat the store also holds keeps its stored door, so only the names are written in. Must fail at **TEAMS equals the store's seats** on multi-seat-collab, naming the missing seats. It may also fail there at **Inbox equals the store's pending asks** and **the post appears on screen**: the held row's worker is no longer a seat, so no worker is on call and the task composer finds no door.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=optimistic-post`: the one send path (`src/lib/send.ts`) reads a line delivered and sends nothing. Must fail at **the post is in the stored transcript** on both Labs. On DevTeam it also fails **the post appears on screen**: the workstream entry's conversation draws a line only once its session holds it, so a line never sent is never drawn. It was reworked for FIX-1792 P4: it used to swap the mailbox transcript module as well, which is gone; the send path it keeps is the one every composer in this goal sends through.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=queued-shown`: Tasks treats no row as queued, so the Queued toggle hides nothing (`src/lib/tasks.ts`). Must fail at **Tasks equals the store's open rows** on DevTeam, whose follow-up row is queued, naming the extra row and the toggle's count.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=unanswerable-asks`: every ask is marked as one the Lab won't reopen, so no card offers an answer. Must fail at **an answer lands in the store** on DevTeam.

The empty Inbox's running count has no control of its own. No Lab reaches an empty Inbox with a running row, so it reads 0. `packages/shift-manager/test/inbox-tasks.test.ts` proves the counting.

**What changed when multi-seat-collab lost its mailbox, and why (BR-24).**

- *The worker seats drain until the row parks.* No worker drains: the planner's board hands the row over at once. The run waits for the stored row to hold an open question (it read `parked` before FIX-2131), and Inbox is graded against the store's pending asks and open questions together, since each is one item.
- *The post on the board-holding workstream, and its reload.* multi-seat-collab has no workstream, so no workstream composer. Its post goes through the task composer into the held row's run session, which is where Shift Manager sends a line for a conversation-board task. The reload half is not graded there: the task frame draws only the task's own run, never the composer's line, so a reload has nothing to draw. The stored half is graded on the run session, and `optimistic-post` still reddens it.
- *PROJECTS, Board, and the answer from the Stream.* multi-seat-collab has no workstream and raises no ask, so these grade DevTeam alone. They were already DevTeam's only in substance: multi-seat-collab's one mailbox had no ask, and its board is now graded through Tasks. DevTeam still runs on mailboxes; what is left for its conversion is that a workstream shows its entry and its owner sees the lead's board, which this goal does not yet grade.

**What changed when the DevTeam lost its mailboxes, and why (BR-24).**

- *PROJECTS and Board on DevTeam.* It has no mailbox workstream, so the sidebar lists none and no workstream Board is drawn; both legs grade it as empty. Its work is graded by **a workstream shows its entry** and **its owner sees the lead's board** instead.
- *The filing.* No mailbox takes a `<issue>: <text>` post. The person opens a workstream led by the EM and files through the EM's `file` door, which takes no model, so the goal stays model-free in what it grades.
- *The post.* DevTeam's post is a turn to the EM in the workstream's session, through the page's composer, graded as one stored user message and drawn once after a reload. With a gateway key set, the EM answers it; nothing graded waits for or reads that answer.
- *The answer from the Stream.* DevTeam's ask sits in the EM seat's own session, which no workstream holds, so it is answered in Inbox with the same card. multi-seat-collab raises none.

**What FIX-1792 P4 removed, and why (BR-24).** Shift Manager has no mailbox workstream level any more: no workstream list in the sidebar, no `/w/<id>` page, no No project. Four legs graded only that level and are dropped with it: **PROJECTS equals the store's mailboxes**, **Board equals the store's rows** (a workstream's Board tab), the mailbox half of the post (a `mailbox-post` in a mailbox's session), and the answer from a workstream's Stream. Both Labs already declared no mailbox, so on HEAD these legs graded nothing. What they protected is graded by **a workstream shows its entry** and **its owner sees the lead's board** (rows on a workstream session's board, drawn at their stored status), **Tasks** (every row and its task frame), the entry composer's post, and the answer from Inbox.
