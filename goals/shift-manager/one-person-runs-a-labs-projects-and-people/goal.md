# shift-manager › one person runs a Lab's projects and people

**Issue:** FIX-1720 (closure of FIX-1650, Org primitives)

**Outcome:** On one `main` commit, a person running the DevTeam Lab alone in Shift Manager asks the chief of staff for two projects, one shared with another member, and finds them under PROJECTS, each with four tabs whose Stream is their own project coordinator for it. They hire through the chief of staff at once and fire only on their own Approve in Inbox. They clear a worker of theirs whose flow a release cut. Every change survives a restart. Someone building the next Lab gets the same from the published docs alone, and every child's own check passes on that commit.

FIX-1793 removed project rooms, and their legs went with them (a3's room line and the worker's answer in it, a4's members-only room, J4's room read, the room in a5, and the docs row for the room's section): the Stream is graded as the person's project coordinator instead.

FIX-1792 took the DevTeam off mailboxes, and a project lists none. Retired under BR-24: D1's "one workstream on each of two teams" in a1 and a2 (a project's workstreams are entries people open in it, which the chief of staff doesn't pick), a3's mailbox workstreams opening from the Workstreams tab, and the mailbox inventory read. The proving legs: a2 and a3 now fail if PROJECTS or the Workstreams tab lists any mailbox under a project; b4 posts in a `feature` workstream the EM leads, through the project entry page, and the line must be kept once in that workstream's session.

**Input:** the DevTeam profile and its tree (`packages/shift-manager/teams/devteam/`) as the children leave it, served by Shift Manager's own command in real Chromium, as the profile's owner, with the second member and the outsider in their own browser contexts. Held out: the two project titles, the workers hired and fired, and leg c's worker and its scratch flow are picked at run time, so no file names them. Every store file is fresh: one for legs a and b across their restarts, one for leg c's three boots, one per control. All are deleted when the run ends.

**Signal:** per step, each compared by id with what the Lab's store holds through its routes, never with Shift Manager's state (FIX-1720 PLAN.md → Checks).

No gap copy on any tab.
- **a4** The second member's Stream on the first project is their own project coordinator, graded as in a3, and not the person's session; the outsider's PROJECTS lists both projects.

**Anti-game:** no assertion on a child's output or on Shift Manager's state. Only rows CoS created in this run are graded; the profile's default projects are left alone and must be unchanged. Every change goes through a CoS turn typed in the Chief of Staff view, a post in a composer, or a click in Inbox: no hire, fire or project block is called by the check. Shift Manager draws no conversation with a person's own worker, so leg c's turn with one is sent as the person over the Lab's routes, the way an app opens a session with a worker; it runs or is refused, and changes nothing. Each CoS turn runs once (FIX-1720 D2); a provider error re-runs that one turn and is reported. The second member's and the outsider's browsers are the same page handed their own user id and verified bearer (the Lab has no sign-in). The Stream's coordinator is graded against the worker the Lab's `projectSetup` names (a read that writes nothing) and the session's own state, never against what the page draws about it.

**Controls:** each runs inside the plain run, on its own fresh store, and must fail its leg at its own step and leave the rest green: every step named below as staying green must run and pass, and a step that never ran counts as red.
