# shift-manager › workers message each other

**Issue:** FIX-1989; legs 6 to 8, FIX-2160 (`specs/issues/FIX-2160/SPEC.md`); leg 9, FIX-2240 (`specs/issues/FIX-2240/SPEC.md` → "How we verify"), landed under FIX-2260; legs 10, 10b and 10c, FIX-2239 (`specs/issues/FIX-2239/SPEC.md`), which replace leg 2b. Its spec's goal: `specs/issues/FIX-1989/SPEC.md` → "The goal, and how we'll know it's met".

**Outcome:** any worker can message any other worker on its user's roster with `sendMessage`, and hears one on `onMessageReceived`. A message lands as its own item, under the sender's name, in the session it is about: an answer (`inReplyTo`) in the session its original was sent from, even a finished task's; a message to the filer of the sender's task in the filing conversation; anything else in the recipient's own conversation. It wakes the recipient, whose plain text reaches no one. One budget per message a person types: 6 messages in all, 3 per request, a refused send spending nothing, and a delegate's ongoing session counting on in its filer's budget. A worker on another user's roster is refused as if it didn't exist, the coder (which hears only in its tasks' sessions) is refused by name before anything is written, a round-robin coordinator is heard, and the person sees every message, both sides, in Shift Manager's Messages view, across a restart.

**Input:**

- Shift Manager's DevTeam install as the commit ships it, served by Shift Manager's own command over a fresh SQLite store (`DEVTEAM_STORE`). Alice is its owner and Bob its second member, each with their own verified bearer, each through the shipped clients (`createWorkforceClient`, the session client, an action client on the flow a session names), loaded from the commit under test.
- The workers: the Shift Coordinator (`shift-coordinator`, the standard coordinator) and Researcher (`researcher`, a standard agent worker), and three Alice hires through the roster flow's `hire`: Helper A (`helper-a`, with Helper B as its delegate, and a favourite colour), Helper B (`helper-b`), the herald (`herald`) and a round-robin coordinator (`rr-lead`, delegate Helper A). Bob hires one worker of his own. Each person's line goes into the worker's own conversation, the session `ensureWorkerSession({ worker })` opens at its derived address.
- The Shift Coordinator routes by best fit. Its conversation's default delegates are removed first, so every line the person sends it reaches its own turn; leg 2 adds Researcher for the task and removes it after. Leg 1b opens the coordinator's second conversation with Alice, and leg 2 files from it, so a message to the filer can be told from one to the default conversation. Routing is FIX-1791's and not what this goal grades.
- Held out, picked at run time: two codenames, Helper A's favourite colour, leg 3b's word, the ring's word and Bob's worker's id.

**Signal:** each failure is tagged `<leg>:<assertion>`.

- **1** (Alice tells Researcher *"Ask the Shift Coordinator what the codename is with a message, without waiting for the reply, and tell me once its answer arrives"* (a send, not a call: leg 10 grades calls), after telling the coordinator the codename): `1:sent` Researcher's turn messages the coordinator; `1:answer-holds-codename` a Researcher answer to the person, in a turn after the coordinator's answer landed, holds the codename; `1:coordinator-shows-message` the coordinator's conversation shows exactly one line *From researcher:* under Researcher's name; `1:coordinator-answered` it sent Researcher a message back.
- **1b** (the other way): `1b:sent` the coordinator, told to tell Researcher a new codename, messages it; `1b:researcher-shows-message` Researcher's conversation shows it under the coordinator's name; `1b:next-answer-holds-it` Researcher's next answer to the person holds it.
- **2** (from a task): the coordinator files Researcher a task to ask Helper A its favourite colour. `2:filed` the coordinator's board holds the task; `2:reached-helper` and `2:from-task` the message to Helper A was sent from the task's own session; `2:answered` Helper A answers it; `2:lands-in-own-conversation` the answer lands in Researcher's own conversation, not the task's ended session, under Helper A's name with the colour; `2:wakes` a Researcher turn writes after it; `2:no-row` Helper A's board holds no row.
- **3b** (through a delegation): Researcher messages Helper A, Helper A files Helper B a task, Helper B's task messages Researcher. `3b:counts-on` that message is counted in the exchange Researcher's line started: the same chain in the log, at a later hop.
- **3** (the limits): `3:per-turn` the herald, told to message everyone, sends 3 and the rest are refused for the per-turn limit; each of Researcher, Helper A and Helper B is told to pass every message on to the next, always, and the ring is started: `3:ring-stops-at-6` the ring's exchange (every message in the chain its first message started, whether or not it repeats the ring's word) holds exactly 6 messages (the limit is the whole exchange's: a worker that both passes a message on and answers it branches the ring, and every branch counts against the same 6; the run stops it at 20 messages); `3:seventh-refused` a conversation shows a send refused at the hop limit; `3:another-round` one more line from the person lets the ring pass at least one more, none past hop 6.
- **4** (refusals, and no delegation): the herald, whose instructions have it call the tool with any id it is given (so the refusal is seen at the tool, not left to a model that skips an id missing from its list), is asked to message Bob's worker and then `eng.coder`. `4:bobs-worker-refused` the message to Bob's worker is refused `no_worker:` and writes no row; `4:create-check` Alice's create at Bob's derived conversation id is refused and Bob holds no session there; `4:coder-refused` a message to `eng.coder` is refused with *file a task*; `4:sender-not-settable` a call naming `from` is never taken, and no row sent from Researcher's conversation names another sender; `4:no-task-row` every row on every conversation's board is one leg 2 or 3b filed.
- **5** (after a restart on the same store, in a browser as Alice): `5:lists-every-message` the Messages view lists every message the legs' tool calls say they sent; `5:both-sides` each with sender and recipient; `5:opens-side` the recipient's and the sender's links of Researcher's first message to the coordinator open those sessions, the recipient's showing the line from Researcher.
- **6** (to a task, FIX-2160; sent by the courier, a hire told to make exactly the call it is handed, so a refusal is seen at the tool): the coordinator's conversation files Researcher a task through the board's own action. `6:lands-in-task-session` a `{ task }` message lands in the session the task's run named, as a line from the courier, and the turn that hears it starts after the task's turn ended; `6:finished-keeps-history` once the task finished, a message asking for a word only the task's goal held is answered from that session; `6:task-unchanged` the row's status, attempts, output and session are the same before and after; `6:not-started` a held task's message is refused `no_task_session` and writes no row; `6:bobs-task` Bob's started task is refused with exactly the text an unknown id gets; `6:own-task` a follow-up running in the task's session, told to message its own task, is refused `not_yourself`.
- **7** (to a conversation by id): `7:second-conversation` Researcher, told to message its second conversation with Alice (opened as Shift Manager opens one), lands there; `7:project-conversation` the courier's message to Alice's Storefront project coordinator conversation lands there; `7:refusals` Bob's conversation (`no_session`), a fired worker's (`no_worker`), a moved worker's (`worker_unavailable`), the sending conversation (`not_yourself`), a task's session and a delegate's session (`not_a_conversation`) are each refused and gain no row.
- **8** (to the coder's task; the coder's scripted run paces its steps at 6 s): `8:finished-answered` a message to the coder's finished answer task (Sandbox, no repository) is answered by a run that resumes the task's coding session, as lines there after the message, with the row unchanged; `8:running-refused` one to its running task is refused `task_running` and writes no row.
- **9** (a message lands once, FIX-2240; `legs-delivery.mts`): each sub-leg has the herald message a worker with a held-out word on a server restarted on the same store with one fault (`faults.mts`), applied through the same `module-patch.mjs` as the controls. Each fault touches only the herald's messages and appends a line to the run's fault log when it fires; a sub-leg whose fault never fired is a setup failure. *Took it* means: the recipient's conversation holds exactly one received item for the id, one `onMessageReceived` request for it got past the take and holds that item, and the row is `delivered` with `deliveredAs` naming that request. The herald is asked for a plain message each time, not a call (FIX-2239), which would park its turn on the reply. `9:crash-after-row` (`exit-after-row`: the process exits after the row, before the dispatch; restarted without the fault): within 60 s Helper B took it, from a request whose dispatch stamp names the herald's session and the pass's `worker-message-resend`. `9:resent` (`fast-path-throws`: the send's own dispatch throws): the tool answered `{ sent }`, Helper B took it from the pass's resend, and `resent.n` ≥ 1. `9:lost-after-accept` (`lose-first-take`: the first request for each id ends before its take): Helper B took it from a later request, and `resent.n` ≥ 1. `9:seen-once` (`late-copy`: each id is dispatched again after the next one lands in the same session; the herald sends two): each id was taken once, and the copy's request completed with no item. `9:crash-after-take` (`exit-after-take`: the process exits after the take, before the items; restarted without the fault): within 120 s Helper B took it from a later request than the crashed one. `9:late-refusal` (`fast-path-throws` and `hold-resend`, which holds the pass's resends before the recipient check until the runner writes a release file): the herald messages `late-helper`, a hire whose conversation Alice opened and whom she then fires; on release the row is `refused` `no_worker`, no dispatch reaches `late-helper`'s conversation in the 30 s after, the herald's conversation holds one *wasn't delivered* line naming the id and `late-helper`, and asked *"Did late-helper get your message?"* the herald says no.

  Where this differs from the spec's table, and why: `9:crash-after-take` allows 120 s, not 60, because the takeover waits for the engine to mark the crashed taker `interrupted` once its heartbeat goes stale (about 60 s after the crash; the copies sent before that find it busy, as they must), then for the next resend on the backoff. `9:late-refusal` fires a hire of its own, not Helper B, so the other sub-legs keep Helper B, and a fired worker is off the roster, so the recheck names it `no_worker` (as leg 7 reads it) rather than `worker_unavailable`.
- **10** (a call; Helper A calls, the run's wait default is 8 s): `10:answer-in-turn` Alice tells Researcher a codename, then tells Helper A to ask Researcher for it with a call; the call's result is the codename, Helper A's answer in the same request holds it, and a request in Researcher's session outputs that reply. `10:no-second-line` Researcher sent Helper A no message after the call, and Helper A's conversation gained no received item from it. `10:task-call` a call to a task Researcher finished answers with the colour only its goal held. `10:default-deadline` (run in 10b's stopped session) a call with no `deadline` answers `running` between 8 s and 8 s plus 6 s after it was sent.
- **10b** (into a session stopped for a person): Researcher's turn stops on `waitForUser` (the leg fails if no stop opened). `10b:queued` Helper A calls with `deadline: 5000` (read from the tool input) and sends one plain message: while the stop is open neither lands in Researcher's session, Helper A's request reads as parked on a wait, the call answers `running` with `recipient: "waiting_on_person"` between 5 s and 5 s plus 6 s after it was sent, and Helper A's turn goes on to answer Alice. `10b:kept` Alice approves; both messages land only after the stopped request ended; Helper A, told only to get the answer, waits again with the call id and gets `answered` with the reply (42), writing no new row.
- **10c**: `10c:one-wait-per-step` Helper A, told to make two calls in one step, does (the leg fails with fewer than two), and one is refused `wait_already_pending`. `10c:not-settable` a tool call naming `call` is never taken, and every row with a call flag belongs to a call some turn's output named.

**Anti-game:** nothing is seeded: every change goes through an action. Messages sent are counted from each turn's own tool outputs (`{ sent: <id> }`), not from the log, so leg 5 grades the log against an independent record. Lines and their senders are read from the conversations' stored items; hop counts and statuses from the log, through the person's roster session. The engine's request read is used only to wait until nothing runs.

**Model:** real. Researcher, the helpers and the herald on `openai/gpt-5.4-mini` (Researcher's file; each hire names it, since DevTeam sets no default model), the Shift Coordinator on `anthropic/claude-haiku-5-5` (its file). Both through `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY`; without one the run is blocked and says so.

**Run:** `pnpm tsx goals/shift-manager/workers-message-each-other/run.mts`. `GOAL_LEGS=1,4` runs some legs (in the order 1, 1b, 2, 3b, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 10b, 10c, 5); `GOAL_LEG9=resent,seen-once` runs some of leg 9's sub-legs; `GOAL_COMMIT=<sha>` serves another commit from its own tree and install. On demand only, never a CI gate.

**Controls:** each is one scratch patch to one Workforce module, applied as the process under test loads it (`goals/lib/module-patch.mjs` through `NODE_OPTIONS`; printed in full by the run, and refused when it never reached the code). A control runs the legs it needs.

- `GOAL_CONTROL=relay-back`: the recipient sends every message straight back to its sender, as the relay did (`messages/message-received-entry.ts`). Leg 1 must fail **`1:no-relay`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-wake`: a message is a line only, never a wake (`messages/message-received-entry.ts`). Leg 1 must fail **`1:answer-holds-codename`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=answer-to-conversation`: an answer lands in the recipient's own conversation, not the session its original was sent from (`messages/send-message.ts`). Leg 2 must fail **`2:lands-in-task-session`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=ongoing-at-0`: a delegate's ongoing session counts on its own, born at 0, and its task's turn starts there rather than at its filing's count (`conversation-board/board.ts`, `conversation-board/task-entry.ts`). Leg 3 must fail **`3:ring-stops-at-6`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-hop-cap`: no budget (`messages/send-message.ts`). Leg 3 must fail **`3:ring-stops-at-6`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=pay-first`: a send takes its place in the chain before the recipient is checked, so a refused send spends one (`messages/send-message.ts`). Leg 3 must fail **`3:refused-pays-nothing`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=fresh-hop-on-task`: a task's session starts a fresh exchange at hop 0 (`conversation-board/task-entry.ts`). Leg 3b must fail **`3b:counts-on`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-roster-check`: a worker not on the sender's user's roster is taken as theirs (`messages/send-message.ts`). Leg 4 must fail **`4:bobs-worker-refused`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=refuse-at-receive`: the send no longer refuses a worker that hears only in its tasks' sessions, so the row is written and the refusal is left to its entry (`messages/send-message.ts`). Leg 4 must fail **`4:coder-refused`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-turn-back`: a coordinator that hands every line on is refused `no_turn`, as before (`messages/send-message.ts`). Leg 4 must fail **`4:round-robin-heard`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-hire-check`: `hireWorkforce` takes a worker flow with no `onMessageReceived` (`workers/register.ts`). Leg 4 must fail **`4:hire-refuses`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=memory-log`: the log is kept in the server's memory (`messages/message-log.ts`). Leg 5 must fail **`5:lists-every-message`** after the restart.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=task-to-conversation`: a `{ task }` message lands in the conversation that filed the task (`messages/message-target.ts`). Leg 6 must fail **`6:lands-in-task-session`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-kind-check`: a `{ session }` target isn't checked for a task's or a delegate's session (`messages/message-target.ts`). Leg 7 must fail **`7:refusals`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=coder-resumes-open`: a message to the coder's open task is sent (`messages/message-target.ts`). Leg 8 must fail **`8:running-refused`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-debt`: the send owes no wake and keeps no owed id, so nothing sends it again (`messages/send-message.ts`). Leg 9 must fail **`9:crash-after-row`** and **`9:resent`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=accept-clears`: an accepted dispatch marks the row delivered and drops the debt (`messages/send-message.ts`). Leg 9 must fail **`9:lost-after-accept`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=window-dedupe`: the take reads neither the row's status nor its taker (`messages/delivery.ts`). The session window FIX-2240 replaced is gone from the code; at a distance past its size it let a late copy through, which this stands in for. Leg 9 must fail **`9:seen-once`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=take-is-final`: a copy never takes over from a taker that ended (`messages/delivery.ts`). Leg 9 must fail **`9:crash-after-take`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-recheck`: a resend skips the recipient check (`messages/delivery.ts`). Leg 9 must fail **`9:late-refusal`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-person-hold`: the admission never reads the person's stop (`messages/queue.ts`). Leg 10b must fail **`10b:queued`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=ignore-deadline`: a call waits for the reply an hour past its deadline (`messages/call.ts`). Leg 10b must fail **`10b:queued`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-default-deadline`: a call with no deadline waits the host's maximum (`messages/call.ts`). Leg 10b must fail **`10:default-deadline`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=send-again`: waiting again sends the message again as a new call (`messages/message-tool.ts`). Leg 10b must fail **`10b:kept`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=relay-the-call`: a call's reply is also sent back as a message (`messages/message-received-entry.ts`). Leg 10 must fail **`10:no-second-line`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-step-guard`: a model step may hold two waits (`messages/call.ts`). Leg 10c must fail **`10c:one-wait-per-step`**.

The spec's other-organization signals (`6:other-org`, and another org's session in `7:refusals`) and its `no-org-check` control are not run here: DevTeam verifies every bearer into its one organization, so no person here has a second one. The engine's `request-sessions.test.ts` covers that refusal.

For FIX-2238, `main` before it (`e2c190e64`) has no `sendMessage`, so every leg fails, `1:own-item` first.
