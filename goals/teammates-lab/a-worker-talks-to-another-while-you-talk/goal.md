# teammates-lab › it a worker talks to another while you talk

**Issue:** FIX-2154 (leg b of the FIX-2152 epic's goal); step 5 and the held-out date amended by FIX-2309, step 5's coding task by FIX-2332, and its project by FIX-2335 (the lab has none); step 4 with FIX-1613
**Outcome:** In the Teammates lab, a list-of-workers app on the Grok Bot pattern, you talk to Chief of Staff, Chief of Staff messages researcher, and researcher's reply reaches Chief of Staff while you are still talking to it; Chief of Staff uses it. Long after both have left Chief of Staff's history window, Chief of Staff still knows them, and the screen shows where its word-for-word reading begins. A coding task Chief of Staff starts runs in the background through Claude Code, in the user's one project, which Chief of Staff puts its conversation in without asking.
**Input:** held out. The launch date is drawn at random each run and written into the working folder's README, which researcher reads and Chief of Staff can't, so Chief of Staff can only state it by hearing it from researcher. Step 4's codename (a colour, an animal and a number) is drawn per run, and its 60 lines are shuffled. The README's typo is one of several (`fixtures/input.json`). The lines you send are in the fixture.
**Signal:**
- **1:** Chief of Staff answers your first line in its conversation.
- **2:** a card *To researcher* in Chief of Staff's conversation, a line *From Chief of Staff* in researcher's, the exchange under *with researcher* in Chief of Staff's Talk panel, and a delivered row Chief of Staff → researcher in the message log.
- **3:** your second line goes in before researcher's reply has landed. The reply shows in Chief of Staff's conversation under researcher's name, after Chief of Staff's answer to you, carrying the date; the log holds it as a delivered reply; a line of Chief of Staff's after it states the date.
- **4:** you tell Chief of Staff a codename, then send 60 more lines, past the 50-turn history window, each answered before the next. Then you ask for the codename and the launch date. Chief of Staff's answer holds all three words of the codename and the date, and Chief of Staff sent researcher nothing new for it (no new card, no new log row). The conversation shows a divider labelled *Earlier turns summarized*, and both the codename line and researcher's reply are drawn above it.
- **5:** you ask for the fix. Chief of Staff starts a coding task with no question: no card asks where, and no project shows anywhere on the page, nor in any worker's line. The coding task's run ends *done*, its steps show Claude Code, and the result lands in Chief of Staff's conversation as the coding task's. On disk: the working folder's HEAD and working tree are untouched, and exactly one worktree of it, under the lab's runs folder, carries a commit that changes README.md alone, fixing its first line and nothing else.
**Anti-game:** Graded on what the browser shows, the message log read through the public client, and git. Never on the lab's adapter or the server's own state. Chief of Staff can't state the launch date without the reply, because it is drawn per run. The disk check reads git, so a run that only says it fixed the typo fails. The step-3 order check reads entry order as drawn, so a reply drawn before Chief of Staff's answer fails. Step 4 counts Chief of Staff's messages to researcher around the question, so asking researcher again for the date fails, and it reads which side of the divider each fact sits on, so a fact still read word for word doesn't count. Each graded list is floored with `tell.over`; an empty log or no worktree counts only where a control produces it, and says so.
**Model:** real — openai/gpt-5.4-mini (Chief of Staff, researcher, writer); coder's run on Claude Code
**Run:** `pnpm tsx goals/teammates-lab/a-worker-talks-to-another-while-you-talk/run.mts` (needs `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY`, and Claude Code signed in or `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`)
**Controls:** each on the same command, failing the steps named and no other:
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-model`: the workers answer on a model that doesn't exist. Steps 1, 2, 3 and 5 FAIL.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-lead-send`: Chief of Staff's `sendMessage` is dropped from its tools (a scratch patch to `message-tool.ts`). Steps 2 and 3 FAIL. Step 5 may fail too: with no way to message, Chief of Staff files its research ask as a task for coder, and after the pick it filed nothing again (2 of 2 runs).
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-reply-tool`: researcher's `sendMessage` is dropped. Step 3 FAILS.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-summary`: the history summary's capability and fold are dropped from the lab's host (a scratch patch to `host.mts`); memory stays. Step 4 FAILS: the divider can't say *summarized*.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-memory`: the summary and memory are both dropped from the host. Step 4 FAILS: nothing holds what left the window.

Step 4 is skipped under `no-model`, `no-lead-send` and `no-reply-tool`: its 60 lines test nothing about the step each control reddens.

Steps 2 and 3 have no isolating red state for Chief of Staff's side: without Chief of Staff's message there is nothing to reply to, so `no-lead-send` reddens both. Under `no-model` the screen doesn't show your own lines either until a reload: a turn that fails shows nothing live.
