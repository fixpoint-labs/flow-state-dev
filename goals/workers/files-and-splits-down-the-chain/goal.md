# workers › it files and splits down the chain

**Issue:** FIX-1802 (epic FIX-1786). Its spec's goal: `specs/issues/FIX-1802/SPEC.md` → "The goal, and how we'll know it's met"; this is its check VG (PLAN.md surface S8).

**Outcome:** any worker whose file lists a delegate that takes a task, on any worker flow, files work for those delegates onto its own session's board, and a worker given a task can split it the same way. Every session in the chain is the owner's, the chain stops at five boards deep or at 100 tasks under one top task (or the app's own cap), and each task's result comes back up, board by board, to the session that filed the top task. A worker with no delegate that takes a task files nothing, by tool or by app.

**Input:**

- A goal-local Lab (`lab/fsdev.config.mts` over the tree in `fixtures/workforce/`) on the real engine and a SQLite store, served over HTTP by Shift Manager's own command. Alice and Bob each sign in with their own verified bearer and act through the shipped clients (the session client and an action client per flow). Four worker flows: the built-in `agent` flow, the coordinator, and two goal-local ones: `foreman`, an app's own worker flow carrying the filing kit (`defineSessionBoard`: the task tools on its one model block, their actions, the board's entries), and `poster`, which takes no task (before FIX-2083, a delegated post and no task).
- The tree: `crew.planner` (`agent`) whose delegates are `crew.one` and `crew.two`; `crew.solo` with none; `desk.router`, a coordinator routing by judgment whose one delegate, `desk.poster`, takes no task; `shop.foreman` on `foreman`, delegate `crew.one`; `deep.d0` to `deep.d6`, each the next one's filer; `wide.top` → `wide.fan`, whose delegates are `wide.leaf` and `wide.other`; `real.lead` on a real model, delegates `crew.one` and `crew.two`.
- Leg a's top worker is Alice's own hire, `lead-<random>`, on `agent`, listing `crew.planner`. Bob hires a worker of the same id, listing only `crew.one`.
- Every turn but `real.lead`'s is scripted by marks in what it reads, and every filing it makes is a call of its turn's own task tools, `createTask` for a filing with an owner, and with `held` for an unassigned one: `[file:<worker>]` files the ask (less that mark) for that worker, `[split:<a>,<b>]` files one piece for each, `[fan:<n>]` files `n` unassigned pieces, `[try-file]` files the ask with no assignee, `[tools]` makes the turn say which tools it was handed. A task's notice is noted and files nothing.
- Leg d's app cap runs on a second Lab whose app calls `hireWorkforce(installation, { taskChainLimit: 3 })` (`GOAL_CHAIN_LIMIT=3`).
- Leg e: `real.lead` on DevTeam's chief of staff's own `model:`, asked in one of the wordings in `fixtures/asks.json`, picked at run time.
- Held out, picked at run time: every goal word, the hired worker's id, leg e's wording. Another wording a person might use for a two-part job must pass too.

**Signal:** each failure is tagged `<leg>:<assertion>`. Every count is read from the store after a 5 s grace.

- **a**: `a:filed` Alice's worker's own `createTask` stored a task for `crew.planner` on her conversation's board; `a:split` that task's session filed two pieces by its own `createTask`, one for `crew.one` and one for `crew.two`, on its own board; `a:four-sessions` the conversation and everything under it is four sessions, all Alice's (the task's session under the conversation, one per piece under that), each row on its own session's board; `a:completes-after-pieces` the top task is `completed` within 120 s of the filing, after both pieces completed, its output the answer of the turn the last piece's ending woke (`Noted.`, FIX-2077 D3); `a:one-notice` Alice's conversation holds one `completed` line for it and acted on one notice; `a:bobs-worker-apart` Bob's worker of the same name is refused filing for `crew.planner` (`unknown_assignee`), files for its own `crew.one` in Bob's own sessions, and is an assignee nowhere in Alice's chain, which holds no session of Bob's.
- **b**: `b:filed` `shop.foreman`, in a session on its own flow, stored a task for `crew.one` on its session's board by its own `createTask`; `b:completed` the task completed in one session of Alice's under the foreman's, and the foreman's session holds one `completed` line for it.
- **c** (`desk.router` and `crew.solo`, each in its own conversation, neither with a delegate that takes a task): `c:reaches-no-delegate` its turn's `createTask` stored its filing on the conversation's own board, every row there is `held` with no assignee, and no task session exists under the conversation. Nothing it files reaches a delegate. Since FIX-2076 every worker's turn has the task tools and an unassigned filing stays on its own board as a step, so the tools on the turn are noted, not graded.
- **d**: `d:depth` the chain `deep.d0` → … → `deep.d6`: `deep.d5`'s task session (on the fifth board) is refused filing for `deep.d6`, by its own `createTask` and by the app's `createTask_tasks`, each `total_task_cap_exceeded`; five boards hold one row each, and no row names `deep.d6`. `d:fan-at-101` `wide.fan`'s task session's turn files 101 pieces: 100 accepted, one refused `total_task_cap_exceeded`, 100 rows on its board, and the app's next filing there refused the same way. `d:app-cap` on the Lab whose app sets its cap to 3: the fan files 4, 3 accepted, the 4th refused `total_task_cap_exceeded`, 3 rows, the app's next refused the same way.
- **e**: `e:filed` `real.lead`'s first turn filed at least one piece for `crew.one` or `crew.two` with `createTask`, on its conversation's board; `e:heard` each piece it filed completed, and its conversation heard the ending once: a `completed` line under the delegate's name, or, for a filing that waited for its answer (`wait`), the tool's own answer.

**Anti-game:** no row, session or notice is written by a fixture: every change is a hire, an action or a line a person sends, or what the system does with them. No drain, `dispatch` or board read from the check while it waits: it waits until the store file holds nothing running. Every grade reads the store file the run wrote, read-only and without app code (`store.mts`: task rows, sessions, requests, items), except what an action answered, which is what the person saw. Leg c's tool list is what the engine handed the turn's model, as that turn said it in its reply, never the prompt. Under the controls, `a:one-notice` goes red beside `a:completes-after-pieces`: that named assertion has no isolating red state (a task that never settles is never heard).

**Attempts:** leg e runs on a real model, so it runs until it first passes, up to three attempts (`GOAL_ATTEMPTS`), each on a fresh store and a fresh Lab; every attempt is printed and the verdict names the one it passed on. Under a control there is one attempt. Legs a to d are scripted and run once.

**Model:** real for leg e: DevTeam's chief of staff's own `model:` (`anthropic/claude-haiku-5-5` at this goal's first commit), gated on a key that serves it (`keysServing`). Legs a to d are scripted and need none.

**Model-free run:** `GOAL_LEGS=a,b,c,d`

**Run:** `pnpm tsx goals/workers/files-and-splits-down-the-chain/run.mts`. `GOAL_LEGS=a,b,c,d,e` runs some legs; `GOAL_COMMIT=<sha>` serves another commit from its own tree and install (`git archive` plus `pnpm install`, kept under the system temp directory across runs). Run one goal at a time, with more than 5000 MB free. On demand only, never a CI gate.

**Controls:** each is a scratch patch to a Workforce module, applied as the process under test loads it (`goals/lib/module-patch.mjs` through `NODE_OPTIONS`); printed in full by the run, and refused when it never reached the code.

- `GOAL_CONTROL=hands-to-a-non-delegate`: an unassigned filing goes to the conversation's only delegate even when that delegate takes no task (`conversation-board/board.ts`, the sole-assignee rule counts the delegates that take none), so desk.router's filing is assigned to desk.poster. Leg c must fail **`c:reaches-no-delegate`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-parent-settle`: a piece's notice never runs for the task that filed it (`conversation-board/task-settled.ts`, the notice entry's admission removed), so the task is never checked after its first turn and stays running on the board above. Leg a must fail **`a:completes-after-pieces`**. `a:one-notice` goes red with it.

**Before-state:** `GOAL_COMMIT=e1ae8e46` is `main` just before FIX-1802's first PR merged (#3003, merge `11c5ba9c`). Every leg must fail there. That commit refuses `delegates:` on any worker but a coordinator, so its Lab serves those standard workers without them (printed at load); a hire that lists them is refused.
