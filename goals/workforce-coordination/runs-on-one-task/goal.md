# workforce-coordination › it runs on one task

**Issue:** FIX-2087, the closure of epic FIX-2056. Its spec's goal: `specs/issues/FIX-2087/SPEC.md` → "The goal, and how we'll know it's met".

**Outcome:** on one `main` commit with every other child merged, a person using a Workforce app on a real model hands out work, waits on it, steers it and hears how it ended through one task; every ending reaches its filer once, after a server restart and when a task fails out too; nothing records an ending except the claim gate or the drain; and no task tool beyond the five (and `answerTask` and `parkOnQuestion` until FIX-2058) is offered, now and in later changes.

**Input:**

- **The Lab** (`lab/fsdev.config.mts`): the concept's cast as `WORKER.md` files only (`cast.mts`, written at run time), on the built-in `agent` and coordinator flows, a SQLite store with durable execution, served by Shift Manager's own command. Alice signs in with her verified bearer and acts through the shipped clients. `desk.launch` (coordinator, routing everyone) hands to `desk.writer`, which asks `desk.researcher` for the fact and fact-checks it in a step of its own in a session of its own (`session: "new"`); `desk.chief` hands questions to `desk.editor`, which files for researcher and waits with `getTask`. The app adds no coordination code. The Lab notes the tools each model call was handed (`GOAL_TOOLS_LOG`), without changing them.
- **Held out, picked at run time:** the topic, the held-out word Alice asks for, the steer word, and the fact, which only researcher's file holds.

**Signal:** each failure is tagged `<leg>:<assertion>`, and each assertion is **structural** or **judgment** (`TAGS` in `run.mts`, printed with every failure; D1).

- **a**, the concept's walk less the call and the Inbox: `a:routed-listed` launch's routed task for writer is in Alice's list; `a:files-research` writer files researcher's task; `a:ends-running` writer's task stays running past its first request and its research; `a:one-settled` one `onTaskSettled` per research ending starts writer again; `a:step-dispatched` writer files its fact-check as a step in a session of its own; `a:drain-waits` the task ends after that step; `a:answers-after-steps` writer gets a turn after the step ends; `a:lands-once` the draft lands once, under writer's name; `a:draft-content` with the held-out word and researcher's fact; `a:follow-up` Alice's follow-up from the finished task runs in its session and is answered by a turn of writer's after it was filed; `a:follow-up-differs` (judgment) that turn writes a new version, not the original word for word (when it does, the two lines read alike and are told apart by when each landed); `a:original-unchanged` the original reads byte-identical; `a:once` every ending reaches its filer once.
- **j1** (a team that hands out work): `j1:routed` (structural) launch routed Alice's message to editor, `j1:files` (judgment) editor filed for researcher, `j1:waits-in-request` editor reads the research's ending with `getTask` in the same request, `j1:read-once` and so no notice turn runs for it (an `onTaskSettled` request that finds the notice no longer owed, output `{ owed: false }`, runs no turn and isn't counted), `j1:heard-once` the ending is heard exactly once, by that read, `j1:answer`, `j1:tools` no task turn is offered a tool beyond the allow list and the roster's own two (`listDelegates`, `sendMessage`), whatever it is called, `j1:once`.
- **j2** (a team that runs a coordinator): `j2:steered` Alice's steer of an open routed task is taken, `j2:steer-shows` the draft carries the steer word, `j2:cancelled` her cancel of another ends it, `j2:cancel-heard-once` launch's session hears it once and no line lands.
- **b**: `b:routed` (structural), `b:files-research` (judgment) writer filed research before the kill; `b:killed` the server dies after researcher's ending is written and before it is sent (FIX-2078's kill point, reused); `b:hears-in-due-time` after a restart with no touch, writer hears it within its window; `b:once`. The window depends on where the kill caught writer's filing request, read from that request's record in the store the dead server left (the research row's `filer.requestId`), never from timing. If the request had ended or parked, the window is the due time plus 2 s, the common case. If it still read `in_progress` (killed mid-turn), the notice is held until the request is known gone: its heartbeat stale past the stale threshold, or the stale or startup sweep marking it interrupted (FIX-2166). With the defaults (10 s heartbeat, 60 s threshold, 30 s sweep) that is about 50 to 92 s after the kill, so the window is the due time plus 100 s. This minute-scale bound is the owner's decision of 2026-10-24; a seconds-scale one is FIX-2169's. A hearing outside either window still fails, structurally. The run prints which case each attempt hit.
- **c**: researcher's model refuses every call. `c:routed` (structural), `c:files-dependent` (judgment), `c:fails-out`, `c:dependent-cancelled`, `c:one-failure-notice` writer hears one failure notice naming the dependent it cancelled, and none for the dependent; `c:writer-ends` (structural) writer's task ends, not left open; `c:once` its one line lands.
- **sweep** (`sweep.mjs`, the engine CI runs): 0 live lines and no CAN'T TELL (every allowed tool read, every recorder key naming one function), and `--control` reports exactly its plants.
- **children** (`children.mts`): every child's goal or check with its controls, the set read from Linear (when it can't be read, row 3 runs the set here and fails); plus the coordinators, hand-offs and FIX-2005 privacy goals, and the goal-seek loop. A control passes only when its goal exits 1, every tag its goal.md says it must fail has a `FAIL` bullet of its own, and the goal prints no diagnostic that a control of its own stayed green. The closure's own findings are children too once filed: each runs its regression tests and names, in `by`, the run here that grades it end to end (a goal another entry runs, a row-1 assertion, or the sweep). A `by` that names no such run fails. A child with an entry here that Linear doesn't list as blocking runs too, and is printed as such.
- **gaps** (`gaps.mts`): the 31 in-scope gaps rows, each mapped to the checks that read it. A row passes only when every check it names ran in the same closure run and passed: a goal or regression test row 3 ran, each control it names red as documented, a row-1 assertion whose leg passed, or the sweep. A label naming no such run, or one not run here, fails. It grades that each row's checks ran green, not that they reach every side of the row; the row's own wording is the claim they're read against.

**Anti-game:** Alice's conversation and rows are read through `listTasks_tasks` and `getTask_tasks` as Alice; every other grade reads the store file, read-only; endings are counted from the filer's notices and requests. No fixture writes a row, a session or a notice. The kill is a real process exit at the delivery's send, and the restarted server gets no action. A structural FAIL on any attempt is a finding, never retried past (D1).

**Model:** real: the goals library's default model through the gateway (`gatewayModel(goalModel())`, `openai/gpt-5.4-mini`), gated on a key that serves it (`keysServing`); without one the walk is blocked, not failed. The sweep and gaps parts are model-free.

**Run:** `pnpm tsx goals/workforce-coordination/runs-on-one-task/run.mts`. `GOAL_PARTS=walk,sweep,children,gaps` (default all), `GOAL_LEGS=a,b,c`, `GOAL_ATTEMPTS=<n>` (default 3, judgment misses only; 1 under a control). The children part runs every composed goal and takes hours. On demand only; the sweep alone runs in CI's Typecheck job (`node goals/workforce-coordination/runs-on-one-task/sweep.mjs`, and with `--control`).

**Controls:**

- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-sweeper-payment` (FIX-2078's, imported from its goal's `controls.mts`) must FAIL **`b:hears-in-due-time`**.
- `GOAL_CONTROL=no-cascade` (FIX-2080's, imported from its goal's `controls.mts`) must FAIL **`c:dependent-cancelled`**.
- Either control leaving its tag green fails the run as `control:broken`, so a broken control can't read as a pass.
- `node sweep.mjs --control` plants tools built through a renamed local alias, a template name, a sequencer and a generator under renamed imports, and a renamed `handler` import and a namespace import in sibling files; a removed name; a removed door brought back in the agent's worker flow and, under a computed key, the coordinator's (a worker flow is a core `defineFlow` declaring `respond` or with a `configSchema` typed by the worker contract, and both built-in flows must be read, or it can't tell); endings through a renamed variable, a claim-passing cancel (called, through `.call`, `.apply` and element access, destructured, and handed on by `.bind`; the same shapes without a claim must not be reported), a renamed, a namespace, a destructured and a passed-on pass-through, destructuring, element access and `.call`, and a direct write, and writes through `.call` and `.apply`, inside a collection wrapper; a second function named like a recorder; and a second list of what waits on the user (FIX-2133: an Inbox item built outside the orchestration read, typed, cast, with shorthand properties, through a spread and untyped). It must report exactly those, and say it can't tell when the real capability's `createTask` isn't built with `handler`. `node sweep.mjs --at <commit>` sweeps another commit's tree: today's `main` before the epic must FAIL.
