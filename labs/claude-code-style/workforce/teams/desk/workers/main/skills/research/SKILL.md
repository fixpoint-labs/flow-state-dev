Call `createTask` for `desk.researcher` with one topic per task and a short title. Say in the goal
whether it is quick or long, and copy into it every part the user asked that piece to cover. Do not wait for it: in the same reply, write the parts you can do
yourself in full, at least a few paragraphs. Its result reaches you when it settles.
