# FIX-1353: W3 — L2 channels as sessions/boards (not Channel L1) — Implementation Spec

## Part I — The Case *(for the human)*

### 1. Problem — why it matters, why now

W3's floor asks for four file conventions. Two of them — rooms and L2 channels — are the
same thing named twice. The rooms lock (FIX-1341) defines a room as what *"binds existing L1
sessions / boards / dispatch"*; this issue defines a channel as what *"composes as
sessions/boards over existing dispatch."* One predicate, two spellings — and the atlas calls
its four channel kinds **rooms**, the direct-message case included.

**Why now** — FIX-1352 is in review and ships the room reader. A second manifest beside it
makes every author guess which folder a thing goes in, with no rule in the tree to answer the
guess. Cheap this week; a migration once apps have both trees.

### 2. Solution in plain terms

Fold this issue into rooms. One convention, one file type, one noun. "Channel" stays a useful
word for what a room is *for*, but it is not a second declared thing, so it gets no file, no
reader, and no folder. The issue's other half — **composition**, a declared room actually
opening a session or carrying a post — is neither dissolved nor blocked; it is out of W3's
scope and owned elsewhere (Decision 2).

**Philosophy** — tenet 3: the surface this adds is zero, the right size for a concept that
already exists under another name. Tenet 2: the invent-kill said no Channel *type*; one level
up, no channel *file*.

### 6. Decisions & rules — the sign-off surface

1. **A room is the L2 channel. This issue ships no `CHANNEL.md`, no reader, and no
   `channels/` folder.**
   *Rejected:* a second manifest sharing the room reader's dialect and identity rules. It
   reads cheap — same walk, different filename — but it buys nothing anyone could name, and
   charges every future author a question the tree cannot answer.
   *Locks in:* one folder for the thing people talk in. Reversible cheaply for about a
   release: while nothing declares a room in files, splitting later is renaming a folder;
   once apps declare rooms, splitting is a migration.

2. **The composition half stays on FIX-1311 and FIX-1341. It does not move into W3.**
   *Rejected:* building the session/board binding here. **Not because it cannot be built** —
   the same-flow group (a board plus one session per subscriber) is marked *exists* on the
   atlas, and the session-opening helper is buildable on today's APIs. It is out on **scope**:
   W3 is the declaration surface, and this is the owner's fence, cleared twice by the
   Architect. What is genuinely unbuilt is narrower — the subscription policy and Collab's
   roster (§7).
   *Locks in:* W3 finishes as three file conventions plus the lab, not four. One gap this
   exposes: the pre-lab DM has no owner today, and §8 step 4 files it rather than assuming
   coverage.

**Non-goals** — a `CHANNEL.md`; a `channels/` slot; a channel reader; binding a room to a
session, board, or dispatch target; the message board; Collab's roster; Channel /
MessageBoard / TeamFlow L1.

**Size:** Zero production code. Six recordings (§8) — one comment, four Linear edits, one
filed follow-up.

### 3. Tradeoffs & alternatives

Two file types (same reader, different manifest name) is what the floor implies; two readers
is its heavier version. Both fail the same test — nothing could say what makes a declaration
a channel rather than a room, since membership, binding kind, and declared-versus-minted each
collapse back into rooms. Two readers also runs into the invent-kill. The alternative that
would have kept this issue's slot is different in kind: give the rooms convention its missing
**consumer**, one helper that opens a session for a declared room. Buildable today, but a
Collab scope call — §12 raises it rather than absorbing it.

### 4. Focus practices

**BP-003, narrowed to what an absence proof cannot show.** §10 dates and pins its absence
proofs rather than stating them as invariants, and keeps them off Decision 2: a missing symbol
shows nothing is *built*, never that something is *unbuildable*. Letting it carry that weight
is how a sound decision acquires a weak premise.

### 5. What using it looks like

One place to put the thing a team talks in:

```
workforce/teams/pentest/rooms/findings/ROOM.md
```

```md
---
description: Where the pentest team posts findings.
kind: group
members: [pentest.scanner, pentest.analyst]
---

Post the finding, the asset it is on, and the severity you assessed.
```

No code reads `kind` today and none needs to — the room reader carries unclaimed keys
verbatim (FIX-1352 §7). What the author does **not** get is a second folder called
`channels/`.

## Part II — The Build Plan *(for the implementing agent)*

### 7. Technical design

**The atlas never proposed a channel manifest.** It names `WORKER.md` 45 times and names
neither `ROOM.md` nor `CHANNEL.md` once. The second file type entered at the epic floor's
wording, where *"L2 channels (sessions/boards)"* reads as a fourth sibling of rooms /
resources / skills rather than as the communication view of rooms. This is the one design
finding that is this issue's own.

**Everything else is already written down.** The record shape a channel file would have
declared (identity from folder names, required `description`, unclaimed keys carried
verbatim, the `{ things, errors }` contract), and the no-caller reasoning that defers
machinery with nothing to consume it, are FIX-1352 §7 and §12. Read them there.

**The composition half: what it needs, and who owns it.** Two of these four are buildable on
today's APIs. They are out of W3 on scope, not capability.

| The ask | Buildable today? | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A DM — one static session with one worker | **Yes** — the session route ships; only the helper is unwritten | **Nobody.** Atlas §16's `openDm` is a proposal, not an issue (§8 step 4) |
| A group — a board plus one session per subscriber | **Yes** — atlas §08 marks same-flow fan-out *exists* | FIX-1311 |
| Subscription policy — who may reply, ordered multi-reply | No — atlas marks it *proposed* | FIX-1311 |
| Seeding, binding, the system/dynamic roster | No — needs Collab | FIX-1341 |

**The atlas is not clean, and this spec does not pretend otherwise.** Ten W3-facing places
still list "L2 channels" as a separate declared thing, and §08/§14 need one vocabulary line
saying a channel is the delivery view of a room. A fold recorded in Linear while the durable
artifact still teaches two conventions is not a fold. This spec PR is never merged (BP-037)
so it cannot carry the edit; it is filed as **FIX-1358**, which enumerates all ten by line
(§8 step 5).

### 8. Implementation sequence

No code ships. Six recordings — one comment, four Linear edits, one filed follow-up:

1. **Answer FIX-1352's §12 open block** (*"rooms and L2 channels may be one convention —
   raised to the epic, not decided here"*) so it resolves rather than shipping unanswered.
   → *Status:* done — the Architect posted the answer on spec PR #1711.
   → *Fallback if it ever needs re-posting and #1711 has closed:* put it on FIX-1352's Linear
   document and issue instead. Once the spec PR closes, Linear is the durable copy and a
   comment on a closed PR is not where the next reader looks.
2. **Correct the epic's floor.** FIX-1351 item 2 ("L2 channels as sessions/boards") is
   removed; item 1 becomes the rooms/channels convention. → *verify:* the epic description
   lists three conventions plus the lab.
3. **Correct the lab's scope line.** FIX-1355 reads *"exercises rooms / L2 channels /
   resources / skills conventions."* Drop the third. → *verify:* the issue description.
4. **File an owner for the pre-lab DM.** The lab needs a static intake DM before Collab
   opens. FIX-1311 owns the group board; FIX-1341 owns the post-lab roster. **Neither covers
   it, and no issue does** — searched, nothing found. Either file it under the epic or fold
   it into FIX-1355's scope; that is the owner's call. → *verify:* an issue exists or FIX-1355
   names it. **Do not close FIX-1353 while this is unowned** — closing on "everything is
   owned" would be closing on something untrue.
5. **Reconcile the atlas — filed as FIX-1358** (child of FIX-1351). It carries the ten
   restatements by line number and the §08/§14 vocabulary line. → *verify:* FIX-1358 exists

6. **Close FIX-1353 as folded**, not as done, pointing at FIX-1352 and §12.

**Removals** — the epic floor's item 2, and FIX-1355's channel clause. Nothing in
`packages/`.

### 9. Edge cases & error handling

**None — there is no runtime here to have edge cases in.** The two ways the *decision* could
be wrong: if the composition half was the primary ask, this is a re-gate of the epic's floor
rather than a design answer (Decision 2); and if a channel later needs a field a room must
not have, it is a key on the room file until a key genuinely cannot express it, so the cost
of being wrong is one key, not one file type.

### 10. Testing strategy

**No behaviours to test — no code ships.**

absence proofs, so they go stale the moment the sibling issues ship — roughly when this issue
closes. They are why the decision was made, not invariants to re-check. **None of them is
load-bearing for Decision 2**, which turns on scope rather than on what exists.

### 11. Documentation plan

**No user-facing documentation change.** No export is added, removed, or renamed and no
behaviour differs, and the rooms page that would carry the vocabulary is already deferred to
its first consumer by FIX-1352's docs plan. The one instruction for whoever writes it is §13.

**The internal atlas does need editing, and it is not this issue's to make.** Ten W3-facing
restatements plus a vocabulary line in §08/§14 — enumerated and owned by **FIX-1358** (§7,
§8 step 5). "No atlas edit required" would have been wrong.

### 12. Dependencies, open questions & follow-ups

**Dependencies.** None; no code ships. One **exit condition**: §8 step 4 — do not close this
issue while the pre-lab DM is unowned.

**Raised to the epic, not decided here:**

- **The consumer reshape (§3)** — one helper that opens a session for a declared room. The
  strongest alternative use of this issue's slot; it crosses the epic's Collab fence.
- **Every W3 convention is built before its only consumer.** The lab (FIX-1355) is sequenced
  last and is the first thing that would declare a room, resource, or skill in files.
  FIX-1352's Decision 1 names this for rooms; it is the epic's ordering, not a rooms problem,
  and it is cheaper to change now that one convention has dissolved.

**Filed:** FIX-1358 (atlas reconciliation).

**Open questions.** None.

### 13. Review notes for the implementer

- **For whoever writes the rooms documentation page** (FIX-1355 or Collab RC, per FIX-1352's
  docs plan): use *room* as the noun throughout, and say in one sentence that a room is what
  the team talks in — so a reader arriving with the word "channel" lands on the right page
  and stops looking for a second one.

## Spec evolution *(the change story)*

- **Spec drafted** — framed as the fold question the epic left open rather than as a channel
  feature. Verdict: one convention, one file type; this issue ships no code, the file half
  folds into FIX-1352, and the composition half stays on FIX-1311 and FIX-1341.
- **After spec review** — direction approved unchanged; the document compressed. The
  three-shape walk became a paragraph, §7 now cites FIX-1352 rather than re-deriving it, the
  predicate-sameness argument is stated once, and §10 keeps three fold-specific checks marked
  decision-time-only. §8 gained a fallback for a closed #1711. All thirteen numbered sections
  stay — the template mandates them, so §4, §9 and §11 are compressed to one real answer each
  rather than dropped.
- **After the premise correction** — Decision 2 previously rested on "the message board does
  not exist." That was a weak premise beside a sound decision: the atlas marks the same-flow
  group *exists*, and the DM helper is buildable today. The decision is unchanged — the
  composition is out of W3 on **scope**, which is the owner's fence — but the reason is now
  the true one, and §10 says plainly that no evidence row is load-bearing for it. Two gaps
  the correction exposed: the atlas still teaches two conventions (filed as FIX-1358, since
  a never-merged branch cannot carry the edit), and the pre-lab DM is owned by nobody (§8
  step 4, now an exit condition rather than an assumption of coverage).
