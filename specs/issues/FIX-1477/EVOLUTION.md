**The first three are not wholly superseded, and the last two are** — those two are factual
claims rather than designs, and a refuted fact leaves nothing to keep. Three of the five are not
designs at all: a sentence in a ticket, a picture, and two statements this spec wrote about
someone else's code. They are here because an implementer who finds them will reasonably treat
them as current, and four of the five would send the work the wrong way.

**Why the last two were wrong is worth more than the corrections.** Both were parsed off the
engine rather than run against it, and the file they were parsed from had already changed. The
cheap guard is the one the corrections used: name the red state, then produce it. Each of the
two corrections above has one, and both are re-runnable — [`poc/read-gate/`](poc/read-gate/README.md)
carries the experiments, the exact commands and the observed output.

Before implementing, compare these against the repository rather than against this page. The
devtool files in particular are live code and may have moved; [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) →
*At implement time* names what to re-check.
